# Off-origin cubic Hermite contact for the longitudinal source

This is an exact additional physical source gate and a retrospective
longitudinal-completion credit.  It does not claim an unrestricted operator
norm bound.  All coefficients below already contain `nu=sqrt(2/pi)`; there
is **no additional nu factor** in the polynomial or its moment price.

## 1. Source normalization

Let `Z` be standard Gaussian independent of the transverse variables `W`.
For odd signs `f,g`, put `h=(f+g)/2`, `k=(f-g)/2` and rotate so `P1h=uZ`,
`u>=0`.  Write

\[
v=\mathbb E[H_3(Z)h],\quad
r=\|P_3^W\mathbb E_Zk\|_2,\quad
Q=P_3^W\mathbb E_Zk/r\quad(r>0),
\]

where `H3(z)=(z^3-3z)/sqrt(6)`.  Choose `A>0`, `B>=0`, and set

\[
L(z)=Az+Bz^3=a z+dH_3(z),\qquad a=A+3B,\quad d=\sqrt6 B.
\]

The pointwise diamond `|h|+|k|<=1` gives

\[
au+dv+r\le \mathbb EJ(Q),\qquad
J(x)=\mathbb E_Z\max\{|L(Z)|,|x|\}.
\tag{1}
\]

For `r=0`, the same argument holds without `Q` and gives the corresponding
source-free head inequality.  The conditional coupling between `Q` and
`h,k` is retained in (1); no independence from these signs is assumed.

## 2. The fourth derivative has the required global sign

Define `G(s)=J(sqrt(s))`, `s>=0`, and let `w=w(s)>=0` be the unique solution
of `s=w(A+Bw)^2`.  Put `D1=A+Bw`, `D3=A+3Bw`.  Since
`ds/dw=D1*D3>0`, the inverse is smooth, including at zero.  Direct
differentiation of the absolute-cubic Gaussian tail gives

\[
G'(s)=\frac\nu2\int_0^1
       \frac{e^{-t^2w(s)/2}}{A+Bw(s)}\,dt.
\tag{2}
\]

For any fixed `tau>=0`, set
`K_tau(s)=exp(-tau*w(s))/(A+Bw(s))`.  Its first three derivatives have

\[
K_\tau^{(j)}(s)
=(-1)^j\frac{e^{-\tau w}P_j(A,B,w,\tau)}
 {(A+Bw)^{2j+1}(A+3Bw)^{2j}},\qquad j=1,2,3,
\tag{3}
\]

where `P0=1` and the exact recurrence, with `r=2j+1`, `m=2j`, is

\[
P_{j+1}=(\tau P_j-\partial_wP_j)D_1D_3
             +rBP_jD_3+3mBP_jD_1.
\tag{4}
\]

The producer expands (4) over the rational multivariate polynomial ring.
For `j=1,2,3`, there are respectively 5,12,22 coefficients, all nonnegative
and at least one positive.  Consequently

\[
G''(s)\le0,\qquad G'''(s)\ge0,\qquad G''''(s)\le0
\quad(s\ge0).
\tag{5}
\]

These follow by differentiating (2) and applying (3) at `tau=t^2/2`.
The integrands and derivatives are uniformly bounded on every compact
`s`-interval, so differentiation under the integral is valid.  Equation (5)
is proved on the complete half-line by exact polynomial signs, not samples.

## 3. A globally valid sextic majorant

Choose any rational contact `w0>0` and set `s0=w0(A+Bw0)^2`.  Let `P(s)` be
the cubic Hermite interpolant matching `G(0),G'(0),G(s0),G'(s0)`.  The repeated-
node interpolation remainder, for every `s>=0`, is

\[
G(s)-P(s)=\frac{G''''(\xi)}{4!}s^2(s-s_0)^2\le0.
\tag{6}
\]

Here `xi` lies between the interpolation nodes and `s`; the same formula
applies when `s` is outside `[0,s0]`.  Equality at a repeated node is obtained
by continuity.  This is why the contact is valid on **all real x**, not only
between fitted nodes.

The coefficients `P(s)=p0+p2*s+p4*s^2+p6*s^3` are explicit.  Put

\[
p_0=\nu(A+2B),\qquad p_2=\frac\nu{2A},\qquad
D=G(s_0)-p_0-p_2s_0,\quad E=G'(s_0)-p_2.
\]

Then

\[
p_4=\frac{3D-s_0E}{s_0^2},\qquad
p_6=\frac{s_0E-2D}{s_0^3}\ge0.
\tag{7}
\]

The last sign also follows from the third divided difference
`G[0,0,s0,s0]=G'''(eta)/3!`.  With `x0=sqrt(w0)(A+Bw0)`, the needed contact
values are

\[
G(s_0)=x_0\operatorname{erf}(\sqrt{w_0/2})
 +\nu e^{-w_0/2}\{A+B(w_0+2)\},\quad
G'(s_0)=\frac{\operatorname{erf}(\sqrt{w_0/2})}{2x_0}.
\tag{8}
\]

Directed Arb evaluation rounds every coefficient upward to an exact rational
`qj>=pj`.  All powers `x^(2j)` are nonnegative, so this componentwise rounding
preserves the global comparison

\[
\boxed{J(x)\le q_0+q_2x^2+q_4x^4+q_6x^6\quad(x\in\mathbb R),
       \qquad q_6\ge0.}
\tag{9}
\]

The frozen head is

\[
A=26412631/10^7,\quad B=4084047/10^7,
\quad a=9666193/2500000,\quad d=\sqrt6\,4084047/10^7.
\]

All contacts and exact rational `q0,q2,q4,q6` are in
`direct_contact_majorants.json`.  In particular contacts `w0=3`, `189/50`
and `17/4` are suitable for the actual remaining `kappa` interval `[2,4]`.

## 4. Moment prices and continuous boxes

For a normalized third Gaussian chaos, the included fresh coefficient-633
theorem supplies

\[
\mathbb EQ^2=1,\quad \mathbb EQ^4=3+\kappa,\quad
\mathbb EQ^6\le15+15\kappa+
 \left(\frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}-\frac{633\zeta}{15}\right)\kappa^{3/2},
\quad 0\le\kappa\le90,\quad0\le\zeta\le1.
\]

The shape is shared with the native scalar profile:
`V=E U^2=kappa*(7/45+zeta/15)`.  Equations (1),(9) give

\[
\boxed{au+dv+r\le A_q(\kappa,\zeta)
=q_0+q_2+(3+\kappa)q_4+
 \left[15+15\kappa+
  \left(\frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}-\frac{633\zeta}{15}\right)\kappa^{3/2}\right]q_6.}
\tag{10}
\]

There is **no extra nu multiplier** in (10).  For each fixed row its price is
affine in `zeta` and convex in `kappa`: `q6>=0` and
`194/sqrt(10)-633/15>0`.  Thus four directed corner prices control a full
`kappa/shape` rectangle.  One row must remain fixed on the rectangle; choosing
the smallest row independently at its corners is not a valid convexity
argument.  Subdivision is permitted when rows change.

## 5. Retrospective longitudinal completion credit

Suppose a certified native or R3 frame uses the longitudinal completion

\[
-b v^2\le2\beta v+\beta^2/b,\qquad b>0,
\]

with `beta` multiplying **normalized H3**.  The uncompleted comparison equals
the completed one minus `b(v+beta/b)^2`.  A fixed row in (10) gives, for
`d>0`,

\[
v\le\frac{A_q-au-r}{d},\qquad
S=A_q-au-r+\frac{\beta d}{b}.
\]

Hence the exact available retrospective credit is

\[
\boxed{-b(v+\beta/b)^2\le
 -\frac b{d^2}\max(-S,0)^2.}
\tag{11}
\]

The sign of `beta*d/b` in `S` is **plus**.  If a frame stores the coefficient
of `(z^3-3z)` rather than normalized `H3`, use `beta_norm=sqrt(6)*beta_raw`.

For continuous rigorous pricing, a safer equivalent is to freeze any
`lambda>=0` on each rectangle before completion.  Add to its saved completed
price exactly

\[
\boxed{\lambda\left(A_q-au-r+\frac{\beta d}{b}\right)
             +\frac{\lambda^2d^2}{4b}.}
\tag{12}
\]

Optimizing this quadratic at a point yields (11), but a sampled optimal credit
cannot be presumed convex across a box.  With fixed lambda, the combined
native/contact `q6` is nonnegative, cumulant price remains convex and shape
price affine, outer `u` price remains a convex quadratic, and the source
chord curvature is unchanged by `-lambda*r`.  Thus existing exact endpoint,
source-chord and tail charges can be reused without a new physical local
cover or scalar half-line comparison.  A globally completed norm proof still
requires every physical source box to pass its correctly assembled price.
