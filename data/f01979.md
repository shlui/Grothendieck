## Frozen centering and source transport

Both profiles freeze their fitting cumulant at \(\kappa_0=53/100\), hence

\[
\theta=\frac{\kappa_0}{3(\kappa_0+2)}=\frac{53}{759}.
\]

This value stays fixed as the source cumulant varies. The required nuisance
covariance contribution has the **plus** sign

\[
+a_2\left[\frac\kappa3-\theta(\kappa+2)\right]
=a_2\frac{2(\kappa-\kappa_0)}{3(\kappa_0+2)}.
\]

The covariance is negative below the fitting cumulant, zero at it, and
positive above it. It is not legitimate to re-center each source point
without rebuilding the local scalar comparison. The nuisance constant has
zero expectation because \(\mathbb E U_\theta=0\).

With \(v_y=y_0/p_2-p_0r_0\), the exact transport

\[
y(r)=y_0+p_0p_2(r-r_0)
\]

keeps \(y(r)-p_0p_2r\), and thus the entire local potential, unchanged.
The transported outer cost is exactly

\[
(b-p_0^2)r^2-2\gamma r+y(r)^2/p_2^2
=br^2-2(\gamma-p_0v_y)r+v_y^2.
\]

The independent replay checks both identities at every priced vertex.

## Expanded price and vertex justification

Let \(\bar q_0=q_0+\epsilon_0\),
\(\bar q_6=q_6+\epsilon_6\),
\(A=\bar q_6+\zeta^2\), and
\(B=G-d-\zeta^2\). Both \(A\) and \(B\) are nonnegative, as verified
exactly for every branch/profile. The price expands to

\[
P=C+u^2-2\ell u+br^2-2(\gamma-p_0v_y)r
+L(z)\kappa+B\kappa^2+H(z)\kappa^{3/2},
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
C={}&6\beta^2/b+v_y^2+\bar q_0+q_2+3q_4+15\bar q_6
+\eta\theta^2-2a_2\theta+6\zeta^2,\\
L(z)={}&q_4+15\bar q_6
+\frac\eta2\left[\frac{1+z}{9}-\frac{2\theta}{3}+\theta^2\right]
+a_2(1/3-\theta)+9\zeta^2-c,\\
H(z)={}&A\left[\frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}-\frac{633}{9}(z-2/5)\right]
+\frac94 Bz^{3/2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The signs of \(A,B\) permit the separately accepted coupled sixth-cumulant
and residual upper bounds. On \(z\in[2/5,1]\), the bracket in \(H\) is
positive. The Hessian in \((u,r)\) is \(\operatorname{diag}(2,2b)>0\).
For \(\kappa>0\),

\[
P_{\kappa\kappa}=2B+\frac{3H(z)}{4\sqrt\kappa}\ge0,
\qquad
P_{zz}=\frac{27B\kappa^{3/2}}{16\sqrt z}\ge0.
\]

Continuity covers \(\kappa=0\). Thus joint convexity in \((u,r)\) and
separate convexity in \(\kappa,z\) justify all polygon/rectangle vertex
bounds. No joint convexity in \((\kappa,z)\) is claimed or needed.
