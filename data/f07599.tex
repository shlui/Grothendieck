\begin{theorem}[A fresh complete scalar norm certificate]
\label{thm:global-b7171-norm}
Let $b_*=7171/10000$ and $B_*=70756627/125000000$.
The dimension-uniform Gaussian sign norm satisfies
\[
 N(b_*)\le B_*=.566053016.
\]
For the explicit reduced rational matrix sequence $H_n(b_*)$
constructed above, with total entry approximation error at most
$n^{-4}$,
\[
 \lim_{n\to\infty}G(H_n(b_*))
 =\frac1{N(b_*)}
 \ge\frac{125000000}{70756627}
 =1.766618976905\ldots .
\]
In particular the last rational number is a rigorous lower bound for
$G_{\mathbb R}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Certificate proof]
Theorem~\ref{thm:same-b7171-regions} bounds every pairing with
agreement amplitude $u\le1381/2000$ or $u\ge31/40$ by a number below
$B_*$.  The remaining closed interval $[1381/2000,31/40]$ has an
explicit partition into $16$ amplitude intervals and $14$ cumulant
intervals.  In each of the $224$ product groups the source intervals
are contiguous from zero to the directed outward bound
$R_{.847}(u_-)$ at its lower amplitude endpoint.  They therefore
cover every physical source, including all cumulants $[0,90]$ and
all tensor shapes $z\in[2/5,1]$.

Each of the $39662$ resulting continuous cells uses one complete
native frame or one of seven independently replayed whole-line
R3 profiles.  The latter include both rank-one and full-even-block
profiles.  The exact transport $y(r)=y_0+E_{02}(r-r_0)$ preserves
the entire local potential.  Its outer price is a fixed rational
quadratic in $r$, plus $u^2-2\ell u$ and a separately convex
cumulant--shape price.  The six matrix gates hold at the original
coefficient and at $b_*$; the constant translation is exactly
$6\beta_{\rm raw}^2(1/b_*-1/b_0)$.
The centering parameter is frozen at the fitting cumulant, and its
variance and covariance corrections are retained at every actual
cumulant.  Thus the original fitting interval imposes no restriction
on these whole-line comparisons.

An independent directed replay reconstructs all $392$ R3 corner
prices forming the $98$ cumulant-band constants, binds all seven
profiles to their complete compact covers and exact tails, and
checks the original and transported matrix gates.  Separate
independent replays check every selected native endpoint price.
An exact verifier reconstructs the transported quadratic
coefficients from the rational profiles, maximizes each source
quadratic including its interior vertex, and verifies the minimum
of the complete native and R3 bounds on every cell.  The maximum is
\[
 \frac{566053014383}{10^{12}}<B_*.
\]
It also checks every partition group, source endpoint and frozen
input hash.  These records are
\texttt{global\_middle\_b7171\_native\_R3\_envelope.json},
\texttt{R3\_corner\_replay.json}, and
\texttt{global\_native\_R3\_envelope\_algebra\_audit.json}.
The middle and two outer amplitude branches cover all feasible
$0\le u\le\sqrt{2/\pi}$.  Replacing $g$ by $-g$ gives the same bound
for the absolute pairing, proving the norm cap.

The Gaussian-to-matrix convergence proved above gives
$C(A_n(b_*))\to N(b_*)$ and $V(A_n(b_*))\to1$.
The total entry error $n^{-4}$ tends to zero, and the exact cloning,
dilation and balancing transformations preserve the ratio.
Hence the reduced rational sequence has actual limiting ratio
$1/N(b_*)$, with the displayed certified lower bound.
\end{proof}
The certified cap $B_*$ is an upper bound for the denominator.
The theorem does not identify that denominator with $B_*$ or the
matrix limit with its reciprocal.
