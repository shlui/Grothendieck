# Accepted native fibre-frame primitives

This package contains complete physical-moment and scalar half-line gates for `P1-(18/25)P3`. It asserts no regional or unrestricted norm cap. Separate directed pricing of source amplitude, cumulant, shape and outer agreement amplitude is required.

Replay all physical partitions and scalar compact leaves/infinite tails without an optimizer:

    python3 code/verify_region.py --output replay.json

The metadata placeholder cap is unused in primitives-only mode. All new continuous norm claims must be assembled from the accepted gates and the mathematical interpolation criterion.
