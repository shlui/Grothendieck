\begin{proposition}[A sharper universal cubic absolute-moment bound]
\label{prop:cubic-rho-star}
For every real Gaussian polynomial $Q$ of pure Hermite degree three with
$\mathbb E Q^2=1$, in every dimension,
\[
 \mathbb E|Q|<\rho_*:=\frac{848521}{10^6}.
\]
Consequently $\|P_3\|_{L_\infty\to L_2}<\rho_*$ uniformly over dimension.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Retain the eight already accepted whole-line Stein comparisons, and put
$\kappa=\mathbb EQ^4-3$, $V=\mathbb E(\|\nabla Q\|^2/3-1)^2$.
For a row with coefficients $(q_0,q_2,q_4,q_6)$ and Young parameter
$\eta>0$, its moment price is
\[
 \sqrt{2/\pi}+q_0+q_2+(3+\kappa)q_4+
 q_6\left[15+15\kappa+\frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}\kappa^{3/2}
       -633\sqrt\kappa\left(V-\frac{7\kappa}{45}\right)\right]
 +\frac\eta2 V.
\]
It is affine in $V\in[7\kappa/45,2\kappa/9]$. At both endpoints it
is convex in $\kappa$, since $q_6>0$ and
$194/\sqrt{10}-633/15>0$. Thus the gap-free eight-interval cover of
$0\le\kappa\le90$ reduces to the same 32 corner prices. A fresh directed
MPFR reconstruction bounds every corner below $848521/10^6$; the largest
is below $0.848520760641951$, at $\kappa=48/5$ on the lower-variance
endpoint. This repricing changes none of the accepted scalar comparisons.
Finally, duality gives
$\|P_3\|_{\infty\to2}=\sup_{Q\in\operatorname{ran}P_3,\,\|Q\|_2=1}
\mathbb E|Q|$, proving the last assertion with the same strict uniform
margin.
\end{proof}
