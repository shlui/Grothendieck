# Exact mixed cubic direction associated with a transverse cubic source

This records an additional physical sign coupling. The identities and completion below are rigorous; no quantified lower bound for the mixed coefficient is asserted.

Let \(Q=I_3(f)\) be a normalized transverse cubic chaos, \(6\|f\|^2=1\), and let \(G=f\otimes_2 f\), a positive semidefinite matrix. Put \(s=\|G\|_F^2>0\) and
\[
 R_2=\frac{I_2(G)}{\sqrt{2s}},\qquad \mathbb E R_2^2=1.
\]
The Gaussian product formula gives the exact projections
\[
 P_2(Q^2)=18I_2(G),\qquad
 P_2\left(\frac13\|DQ\|^2-1\right)=12I_2(G).
\]
Thus
\[
 \mathbb E[Q^2R_2]=18\sqrt{2s},\qquad
 \mathbb E[UR_2]=12\sqrt{2s}.
\]
Using the included fourth-moment identities
\(\kappa=1296s+1944h\) and \(V=288s+216h\),
\[
 s=\frac{V-\kappa/9}{144},\quad
 \|P_2(Q^2)\|_2^2=\frac92(V-\kappa/9),\quad
 \|P_2U\|_2^2=2(V-\kappa/9).
\]
If \(D=1+3h/(2s)\), this also gives
\(\mathbb E[Q^2R_2]=\sqrt{\kappa/(2D)}\).

For independent longitudinal \(Z\), the product \(ZR_2(W)\) is a unit vector in the full third Gaussian chaos, orthogonal to \(H_3(Z)\). For actual sign half-sum \(h_0=(f_0+g_0)/2\), define
\[
 v=\mathbb E[H_3(Z)h_0],\qquad
 t=\mathbb E[ZR_2(W)h_0].
\]
Consequently
\[
 \|P_3 h_0\|_2^2\ge v^2+t^2.
\]
For every \(b>0\) and real \(\beta,\gamma\),
\[
 -b\|P_3h_0\|_2^2\le -2\beta v-2\gamma t+\frac{\beta^2+\gamma^2}{b}.
\]
A local longitudinal completion therefore admits the enlarged head
\[
 L(Z,W)=\ell Z+\beta H_3(Z)+\gamma ZR_2(W),
\]
with outer constant \((\beta^2+\gamma^2)/b\). The existing completion keeping only \(H_3(Z)\) discards \(-bt^2\).

There is also an exact physical support inequality. If \(|h_0|+|k_0|\le1\), \(u=\mathbb E Zh_0\), and \(r=\langle Q,k_0\rangle\), then for arbitrary real \(a,d,e\),
\[
 au+dv+et+r\le
 \mathbb E\max\{|aZ+dH_3(Z)+eZR_2(W)|,|Q(W)|\}.
\]
It retains the joint \((Q,R_2)\) law. Pricing this expectation by treating \(Q,R_2\) as independent would lose the exact covariance displayed above.

The source direction \(R_2\) is a centered positive quadratic Gaussian form. In particular,
\(R_2\ge-\operatorname{tr}G/\sqrt{2s}=-1/(6\sqrt{2s})\).
No sharp Gaussian upper bound for its \(L^1\) norm has been assumed here.

For the simplified reflected threshold
\(h_0=\operatorname{sign}(\ell Z+\beta H_3(Z))\mathbf1_{|\ell Z+\beta H_3(Z)|>r|Q|}\), if this head is monotone then
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[Zh_0]=c\exp[-z(Q)^2/2],\qquad
 \ell z(Q)+\beta H_3(z(Q))=r|Q|.
\]
The small-source expansion has leading mixed coefficient
\(-c[r/(\ell-3\beta/\sqrt6)]^2\mathbb E[Q^2R_2]/2\).
This expansion is only a guide: the actual native local optimizer includes additional even longitudinal terms in the disagreement potential, so a global norm proof must price its full joint potential and quantify the remainder.
