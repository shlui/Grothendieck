# A support, mass, and cubic-source bridge

This is a proved dimension-independent inequality conditional only on the
new uniform cubic absolute-moment premise

\[
 \mathbb E|Q|\le\rho,\qquad \rho=849/1000,
 \qquad Q\in\mathcal H_3,\quad\mathbb E Q^2=1.
\]

The parent investigation supplies the complete replay of that premise. The
inequalities below do not constitute an operator-norm certificate for
\(P_1-(18/25)P_3\). Bridge values are evaluated with directed MPFR arithmetic;
the recovered discovery-profile comparisons are only a screening diagnostic.

Let \(Z\) be a standard Gaussian independent of the Gaussian vector \(W\),
let \(|h|+|k|\le1\), and put

\[
 c=\sqrt{2/\pi},\qquad u=\mathbb E Zh\in[0,c],\quad
 p=\mathbb E|k|,\qquad
 r=\|P_3^W\mathbb E_Z k\|_2.
\]

The source is the **transverse** cubic projection. The same argument cannot
be applied to a normalized full cubic projection that depends on \(Z\),
because the needed independence would fail.

For \(a>0\), define

\[
 J_a(y)=\mathbb E_Z\max\{a|Z|,y\}
 =ac\exp[-y^2/(2a^2)]+y\operatorname{erf}(y/(\sqrt2a)),\quad y\ge0.
\]

Then, for every \(a>0\) and every \(\lambda\ge0\),

\[
 \boxed{au+r+\lambda p\le
 (1-\rho^2)J_a(\lambda)+\rho^2J_a(\lambda+1/\rho).}
 \tag{1}
\]

Thus the support/source bound can retain the actual disagreement mass,
instead of separately combining a source norm bound and a mass bound.
The exact constants in this bridge are

\[
 \rho^2=720801/1000000,\qquad
 1-\rho^2=279199/1000000,\qquad 1/\rho=1000/849.
\]

## Proof of the scalar price

Write \(F(y)=J_a(y+\lambda)\) and \(x=1/\rho\). Its derivatives satisfy

\[
 F'(y)=\operatorname{erf}((y+\lambda)/(\sqrt2a)),\qquad
 F'''(y)=-\frac{c(y+\lambda)}{a^3}
               e^{-(y+\lambda)^2/(2a^2)}\le0.
\]

Let \(D=F(x)-F(0)\), and form the quadratic

\[
 H(y)=F(0)+\beta y+\gamma y^2,\quad
 \beta=2D/x-F'(x),\quad
 \gamma=[xF'(x)-D]/x^2.
\]

It agrees with \(F\) at \(0,x\) and with \(F'\) at \(x\).
The Hermite interpolation remainder is
\(F'''(\xi)y(y-x)^2/6\); hence \(F(y)\le H(y)\) for all \(y\ge0\).
Also \(F'\) is nonnegative, increasing, and concave on this half-line.
Consequently \(D\ge x[F'(0)+F'(x)]/2\), so \(\beta\ge0\).
The inequality \(D\le xF'(x)\) also gives \(\gamma\ge0\).

For any \(Y\ge0\) with \(\mathbb EY^2=1\) and
\(\mathbb EY\le\rho\), therefore,

\[
 \mathbb EF(Y)\le F(0)+\beta\rho+\gamma
 =(1-\rho^2)F(0)+\rho^2F(1/\rho). \tag{2}
\]

Equation (2) is sharp for this abstract mean/variance class: take
\(Y=1/\rho\) with probability \(\rho^2\), and zero otherwise. This
abstract sharpness says nothing about realizability by Gaussian cubic
chaos; cubic fourth and sixth moments can strengthen the price.

When \(r>0\), normalize \(P_3^W\mathbb E_Zk\) to a variance-one cubic
\(Q(W)\). Then \(\mathbb E Qk=r\). Pointwise,

\[
 aZh+Qk+\lambda|k|
 \le a|Z||h|+(|Q|+\lambda)|k|
 \le\max\{a|Z|,|Q|+\lambda\}.
\]

Independence gives its expectation as \(\mathbb EJ_a(|Q|+\lambda)\).
Applying (2) proves (1). The zero-source case follows by applying the
same support comparison to any variance-one cubic source and observing
that the source pairing is then zero. An unused Gaussian coordinate
may be adjoined if necessary.

No claim \(\mathbb E Qh=0\) is used or needed.

## Closed source-only bound

At \(\lambda=0\), let

\[
 A_\rho(a)=(1-\rho^2)ac+\rho^2J_a(1/\rho).
\]

Its derivative is

\[
 A_\rho'(a)=c\{1-\rho^2+\rho^2e^{-1/(2a^2\rho^2)}\}.
\]

It follows by minimizing \(A_\rho(a)-au\) that

\[
 r\le R_\rho(u):=
 \begin{cases}
 \rho,&0\le u\le c(1-\rho^2),\\
 \rho\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\sqrt{-\log v}\right),
 &c(1-\rho^2)<u<c,\\
 0,&u=c,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad v=\frac{u/c-(1-\rho^2)}{\rho^2}.
 \tag{3}
\]

In the middle case the exact optimizer is
\(a_*=1/(\rho\sqrt{-2\log v})\). The endpoint cases follow by
\(a\downarrow0\) and \(a\uparrow\infty\), respectively. This is a
complete whole-amplitude source gate. It improves the abstract separate
bound \(\min\{\rho,m(u)\}\), where
\(m(u)=\operatorname{erf}(\sqrt{\log(c/u)})\), but the recovered
moment-priced source rows are stronger at the two critical amplitudes
checked in `comparison.json`.

For the mass-price gate (1), its smooth stationary conditions have a
useful explicit parametrization. Set
\(s_0=\lambda/a\) and \(s_1=(\lambda+1/\rho)/a\), so
\(0\le s_0<s_1\). Then

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 u/c&=(1-\rho^2)e^{-s_0^2/2}+\rho^2e^{-s_1^2/2},\\
 p&=(1-\rho^2)\operatorname{erf}(s_0/\sqrt2)
       +\rho^2\operatorname{erf}(s_1/\sqrt2),\\
 r&=\rho\operatorname{erf}(s_1/\sqrt2),\\
 a&=1/[\rho(s_1-s_0)],\qquad
 \lambda=s_0/[\rho(s_1-s_0)].
 \end{aligned} \tag{4}
\]

For the middle range \(c(1-\rho^2)<u<c\), the \(\lambda>0\) part activates
when the actual disagreement mass is above the unpriced extremizer's mass
\(p_0=\rho R_\rho(u)\). In the low flat range \(0<u\le c(1-\rho^2)\),
the corresponding threshold instead is
\(p_{\rm flat}(u)=\rho^2+(1-\rho^2)\operatorname{erf}\sqrt{\log(c(1-\rho^2)/u)}\).
At the maximal admissible mass \(p=m(u)\), the limiting gate is
\(r\le\rho m(u)\). This uses the support mass; it is not the same
as multiplying two unrelated projection estimates.

## Relation to the mixed energy budget

The inherited mixed energy coefficients are exactly

\[
 A=2403891349687/2000000000000,\qquad
 B=12706580435619/10000000000000.
\]

For signs \(f=h+k,g=h-k\), one still has

\[

bound. Further geometry must connect the transverse source, the other
cubic fibers, and the same support set.
