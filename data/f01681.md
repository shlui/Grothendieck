## Shape-dependent `J_a` source price

The argument in `tonight/moments/Ja_SOURCE_SHAPE_PROOF.md` passes.
The transverse normalized cubic is independent of the longitudinal Gaussian,
and its pairing with `k` equals the source norm. The pointwise diamond
constraint therefore gives `a u + r ≤ E J_a(Q)`.

Symmetry gives `J_a(x) = E|x+aZ|`; its Gaussian expectation is
`c sqrt(1+a²)`. The displayed derivative of the odd Stein solution has
the correct normalization at zero and the correct Mills-ratio terms.
Its upper sign follows from the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck representation, since
`J_a'' ≥ 0`; the lower tail bound follows from `0 ≤ G(x) ≤ x^−2`.

The clipped Young function is exactly
`sup_{v≥−1}(−v t − η v²/2)`. Thus it uses the true constraint
`U = τ−1 ≥ −1` with the correct sign. Its quadratic branch dominates
the clipped value everywhere, so using the quadratic penalty when a
branch cannot be selected remains safe.

The affine coefficient of `V` is exactly
`η/2 − 633 sqrt(κ) q6`. The source and native operator price must use
the same `κ,V` of the same transverse cubic. Independently maximizing
the source over `V` and then imposing its optimizer on the native price
would be invalid; the current note correctly preserves the shared shape.
The fitting value `κ=.53` is not a restriction on a whole-line scalar
majorant once that majorant has been certified.

## Physical cubic profile restrictions

The support rearrangement and conditional first-moment bound in
`tonight/theory/physical_cubic_profile_constraints.md` have the correct
directions. In particular the function `F` is decreasing and concave,
so Jensen yields the stated upper bound on `E|k0|`. This conditional
mean is not the support mass; replacing the latter by the former in a
nonnegative mass price only weakens the inequality and is safe.

The normalized longitudinal Hermite expansion gives exactly the stated
conditional variance polynomial, because different transverse chaos
degrees are orthogonal. The piecewise function `Ψλ(A,s)` is a concave
majorant of `max(A,sqrt(s))−λ sqrt(s)` and joins its two branches with
matching first derivative. Conditional Jensen therefore proves the
full-source relaxation without assuming independence.

The first variation at a transverse source has vanishing longitudinal
odd channels. The degree-two contribution is
`−sqrt(2)/a E[R1 Q0 φ(Q0/a)]`, with the displayed Gaussian density
normalization. Its related derivative of the first projection of the
independent-source conditional mean is also correct. Chaos orthogonality
does not force this nonlinear first projection to vanish.

## Extra positive longitudinal channels

The Schur-complement barrier in
`tonight/operator/coupled_stein/EXTRA_LONGITUDINAL_CHANNEL_BARRIER.md`
is valid for the stated family. Eliminating a positive uncharged tail
block preserves each charged domination gate, and the added source
cost changes by exactly `a_t^T C^−1 a_t`.

For every actual local function, the extended expression plus its scalar
source cost is the reduced expression plus a nonnegative square. Hence
an accepted extended majorant transfers to a reduced one by adding
`a_t^T C^−1 a_t t²`; the outer value is unchanged. Eliminating the odd
tail contributes only nonnegative squares. This establishes a limitation
of the positive Gram-completion family, including an unrestricted
quadratic-price coefficient. It makes no claim about stronger constraints
or different higher-chaos operators.

No substantive mathematical gap was found in these three notes. Their
stated remaining requirement—a complete all-law operator certificate—
is essential.
