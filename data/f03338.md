# Cubic absolute-moment caps from the accepted complete Stein rows

The eight accepted scalar rows, the fresh coefficient-633 cumulant theorem, and the standard third-chaos Stein identity imply the stronger universal bound

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E|Q|<\rho_*:=848515/10^6=.848515}
\]

for every normalized real cubic Gaussian chaos in every finite dimension. This statement uses no inherited historical Grothendieck norm premise. It is a repricing of complete, already accepted whole-line scalar comparisons, rather than a new sampled scalar fit. It does not prove a global Grothendieck operator cap.

## Price with the actual cumulant and coupled shape

Let `kappa=E Q^4-3`, `U=||DQ||^2/3-1`, and put

\[
\mathbb E U^2=\kappa(7/45+s/15),\qquad 0\le s\le1.
\]

For accepted row `i`, let `q0_i,q2_i,q4_i,q6_i,eta_i` be its frozen rational coefficients. Every row has `q6_i>0` and `eta_i>0`. Its scalar inequality is valid on the entire real line and therefore its moment price is valid for every `0<=kappa<=90`; the interval where it was originally fitted or priced is not a restriction on the scalar inequality. The explicit price is

\[
L_i(\kappa,s)=c+q_{0,i}+q_{2,i}+(3+\kappa)q_{4,i}
 +\{15+15\kappa+(K-633s/15)\kappa^{3/2}\}q_{6,i}
 +\eta_i\kappa(7/90+s/30),
\]

where `c=sqrt(2/pi)` and `K=194/sqrt(10)`. Hence

\[
\mathbb E|Q|\le\min_i L_i(\kappa,s).
\]

For a **fixed row**, this price is affine in `s` and convex in `kappa`, because `q6_i>0` and `K-633/15>0`. A rectangle is consequently controlled by its four endpoint prices. One may choose a different row on each rational rectangle. The pointwise minimum of the prices need not be convex; the certificate does not assume it is.

The 2,780 rational cumulant leaves in `kappa_shape_caps.json` exhaust `[0,90]` without gaps, select one accepted row on each leaf, and cover the full shape interval `[0,1]`. Their largest outward rational cap is

\[
848514861689/10^{12}=.848514861689<\rho_*.
\]

This proves the displayed universal theorem. The associated projection consequence is `||P3||_(Linfinity->L2)^2 <=rho_*^2`, by normalizing a nonzero cubic projection and applying duality.

## Inserting the shape price into a native profile

If a certified native scalar row has nonnegative coefficient `mu` on the separate cubic L1 bound, replace its term `mu*(rho-c)` by `mu*(L_i(kappa,s)-c)`. This needs no new local or scalar half-line comparison: it only strengthens the independent expectation bound. The combined sixth coefficient is `q6_native+mu*q6_i>0`, and the combined variance coefficient is `(eta_native+mu*eta_i)/2>0`. The price remains convex in cumulant and affine in shape on any box where the same two rows are used, so the same four-corner reasoning applies. Subdivide the cumulant domain where different L1 rows are selected.

For the transverse source bridge, a uniform cumulant-bin cap may replace the universal `rho`, giving the corresponding `Rrho(u)` bound for that bin. Any changed source-grid endpoint still needs explicit coverage or interpolation; a smaller source cap does not by itself validate a new endpoint price.
