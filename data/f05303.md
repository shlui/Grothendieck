## Spectral and local comparison

For odd signs \(f,g\), put \(h=(f+g)/2\), \(k=(f-g)/2\), and rotate so \(P_1h=uZ\). Let \(W\) be the transverse Gaussian coordinates. Write \(k_j(W)=\mathbb E_Z[H_j(Z)k(Z,W)]\), \(h_j(W)=\mathbb E_Z[H_j(Z)h(Z,W)]\), where the four \(H_j\) are orthonormal Hermites. Let \(r=\|P_3^Wk_0\|_2\), and \(Q=P_3^Wk_0/r\) when \(r>0\).

Let \(M\) have even block \(E\) on coordinates \(0,2\) and odd block \(R\) on coordinates \(1,3\). The sufficient spectral gates are

\[
 E\succeq0,\quad E-\operatorname{diag}(-1,b)\succeq0,
 \quad R\succeq0,\quad R-\operatorname{diag}(-1,b)\succeq0,
 \quad R-\operatorname{diag}(b,0)\succeq0.
\]

For arbitrary real \(\ell,\beta,\delta,\nu\), define

\[
 \Gamma=\frac{(\nu-E_{02})^2}{E_{22}}-E_{00}+b-2\delta,
 \qquad s=(\delta,0,-\nu,0).
\]

Projection onto each transverse chaos, the even-block Schur complement, and the longitudinal completion \(-b(\mathbb Eh_3)^2\le2\beta\mathbb Eh_3+\beta^2/b\) give

\[
 \langle f,(P_1-bP_3)g\rangle\le
 u^2-2\ell u+\mathbb E\Phi(rQ)+\Gamma r^2,
\]

where a sufficient local potential is

\[
 \Phi(t)=\sup_{h,k}\left\{k^TMk+2t\,s^Tk
                  +2\ell h_1+2\beta h_3+\frac{\beta^2}{b}\right\}.
\]

The conditional functions satisfy \(|h|+|k|\le1\). The spectral comparison needs no additional gate involving \(\delta\).

\[
