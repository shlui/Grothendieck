# Independent quartic physical-budget review

The analytic inequalities in
`tonight/theory/physical_profile_mixed_completion.tex` pass independent audit.
They are physical restrictions on actual functions, not assumptions that an
arbitrary moment vector is realizable.

For `|h|+|k|≤1`, put `A=E Zh`, `p=|E k|`, `ν²=2/π`, and `C=1−ν²`.
The rearrangement argument is valid even if `E|k|>p`: its comparison adds
the nonnegative term `t(E|k|−p)`. The sharp bound is
`|A|≤ν exp(−t²/2)` at `p=2Φ(t)−1`, attained by the displayed disjoint
central/tail functions.

The quartic inequality `A²+p²−Cp⁴≤ν²` has a correct single-crossing proof.
Direct differentiation gives `D'=2p'g` and `g'=p'(t²−6Cp²)`. The ratio
`t/p` is strictly increasing because `p>tp'` for every positive `t`.
Its endpoint value satisfies `1/ν²<6C`; the limit of `g` is
`1−2C>0`. Thus `g` initially decreases, then increases through exactly
one further zero. Consequently `D` decreases and then increases between
two zero endpoint limits, proving `D≤0`. The improvement over the
ellipse is exactly `C p²(1−p²)`.

For a normalized physical cubic `Q=I3(F)`, the actual quadratic source
`R2=I2(G)/sqrt(2s)` is centered and unit. The product coefficient is
`P2(Q²)=18 I2(G)`, so `θ=E Q²R2=18 sqrt(2s)` and
`θ²=κ/(2D)`. There is no missing Wick-factor or symmetric-pair factor.
The scalar square completion gives
`−b t²≤w E(A−a−cQ²)²−bw c² θ²/(b+w)` with the stated sign.
Expanding the exact fourth moment produces the displayed outer constant.
The direction `Z R2(W)` is unit third chaos and orthogonal to the pure
longitudinal cubic, so both negative projection-energy prices may be
retained together. Its dependence on the same physical source is allowed;
no independence from `h` or `k` is claimed or needed.

The insertion's scalar variable must be the value of the **normalized**
`Q`. If the existing local variable `t=r|Q|` is used, the coefficient
`c q²` becomes `c t²/r²` and the curvature allowance changes accordingly.
I reported this notation issue to the producer. The new chord allowance
`2w|c|ν(q−q0)(q1−q)` is correct in the normalized convention: adding
`2w|c|ν q²` makes each physical candidate convex. All pre-existing
quadratic-source allowances must remain.

The quartic local cost does require a new complete local enclosure.
A direct extension of the current physical-moment box method is possible.
For `F(y)=−w y²+wC y⁴`, linearize at the box center `c0`. Insert
`F'(c0)/2` into the support polynomial's `k0` coefficient and add the
constant `F(c0)−c0F'(c0)`. Since
`F''(y)≤w(12C−2)` on `|y|≤1`, add
`w(6C−1) width0²` to the old quadratic box radius. This yields an
upper bound for the entire physical box; the coefficient is positive
for `C=1−2/π`. Reusing the old quadratic cover without this or another
quartic enclosure would be invalid.

No substantive analytic error was found. The proposal still needs its
modified local/source-domain and outer-law certificates before it can
support a new operator-norm claim.
