# Three original Gaussian variables and a diffuse cubic channel

This is an adversarial **floating discovery screen**, not a norm certificate.
No stable value above `40/71 = 0.5633802816901408...` was found.  In particular,
these files neither prove nor disprove a global `1.775` Grothendieck lower bound.

## Exact reduced objective

Let `X=(X1,X2,X3)` and `Y` be independent standard Gaussian variables.  The
intended original-degree interpretation is that `Y` is the Gaussian limit of a
diffuse normalized original-degree-three chaos.  Thus `Y` has original degree
three, even though it is a linear coordinate in this reduced Gaussian model.
The existing sign-witness original-degree accounting is in Appendix
`Original-degree accounting for the sign witness` of
`reduced_search/deliverables/Grothendieck_reduced_sequence_2026-10-01.tex`.
This screen does not replay that argument or certify a finite CLT error.

Use normalized probabilists' Hermite functions

```
H1(x)=x,
H2(x)=(x^2-1)/sqrt(2),
H3(x)=(x^3-3x)/sqrt(6).
```

The basis contains all three first-degree terms and all ten third-degree terms
in the original coordinates.  Its order is fixed by the multiindices

```
(1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1),
(3,0,0), (0,3,0), (0,0,3),
(2,1,0), (2,0,1), (1,2,0),
(0,2,1), (1,0,2), (0,1,2), (1,1,1).
```

Here `H_alpha(X)=prod_j H_{alpha_j}(Xj)`, with `H0=1`.  For separate
13-coefficient polynomials `q_f,q_g`, take

```
f=sign(q_f(X)+Y),
g=sign(q_g(X)-Y).
```

Analytic integration in `Y` gives

```
m_alpha(q)=E[H_alpha(X) erf(q(X)/sqrt(2))],
a(q)=sqrt(2/pi) E[exp(-q(X)^2/2)].
```

The reduced `P1-b P3` bilinear pairing is exactly

```
B_b = sum_{|alpha|=1} m_alpha(q_f)m_alpha(q_g)
      - b sum_{|alpha|=3} m_alpha(q_f)m_alpha(q_g)
      + b a(q_f)a(q_g).
```

The last sign is positive because `E[Yg]=-a(q_g)`.  The optimizer uses the
analytic derivative, for each coefficient of `q_f`,

```
2 E[H_alpha(X) phi(q_f(X))
     (sum_beta w_beta m_beta(q_g)H_beta(X)-b a(q_g)q_f(X))],
```

where `w_beta=1` at degree one and `w_beta=-b` at degree three.  The reflected
case `q_f=q_g` uses twice this derivative.  A floating Hermite Gram check has
maximum error `1.12e-15`; an independent finite-difference gradient check has
maximum error `3.28e-10`.

## Search and verification

For each of `b=.72` and `.7171`, there were **84 starts**, comprising 28
reflected-symmetric and 56 asymmetric starts.  The first pass used GH28 with
24 starts.  The second used GH36 with 60 starts, including independent generic
cubic coefficients, cubic-antipodal seeds, and seeds far from the known
rank-one reflected family.  L-BFGS-B bounds were `[-12,12]` on every coefficient.
This is a bounded local search and provides no exhaustive assertion.

The strongest coarse candidates were optimized at GH56 or GH72.  Crucially,
**every one of the 168 coarse local optima**, rather than only the top few on
their discovery grid, was also evaluated at GH96.  The strongest of these
were refined there and checked at GH128 and GH160.  Separate streamed
integration builds the basis using `eval_hermitenorm`, does not import the
discovery engine, and checks selected stable and suspicious candidates at
GH192, GH256 and GH320.  Randomized Sobol checks are a supplementary numerical
comparison.  Neither quadrature agreement nor the Sobol estimates supply
rigorous integration error bounds.

The stable best candidates reduce, after an original-coordinate rotation, to
the existing one-dimensional reflected threshold:

| b | q(Z)=A Z+D H3(Z) | Streamed GH320 pairing | Gap below 40/71 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| .72 | A≈3.730753795, D≈.961719282 | .5632418877560343 | .0001383939341065 |
| .7171 | A≈3.866477192, D≈1.000383118 | .5632335247976815 | .0001467568924593 |

For the `.72` candidate, the reduced first amplitude is approximately
`.747698156854`, the original cubic amplitude `.267698217365` (negative in the
aligned `H3` direction), and the diffuse cubic amplitude `.278353880508`.
For `.7171`, these are `.750515528144`, `.270788461099`, `.270685360072`.
The asymmetric optimizers converge to equal `q_f,q_g`; cubic components
orthogonal to the rotated rank-one cubic are at most a few `1e-7` in these
stable best candidates.  These are descriptive floating observations.

Several larger coarse values were artifacts.  For example, a `.7171` GH56
value `.5661802132` decreased to `.5639187672` at GH80, `.5615466928` at GH144,
and `.5608000652` at GH320.  In the broader pass, apparent GH72 values
`.5642791437` and `.5645962386` decreased to `.5603916827` and `.5606874299`
at GH320.  Such discrepancies are why coarse maxima are not reported as
counterexamples.

The reciprocals of the stable floating pairings are approximately
`1.7754361345` and `1.7754624964`.  These are estimated witness ceilings for
the asymptotically unit vector numerator, **not certified lower endpoints**.
The screen found no new adversarial shape and no accepted obstruction.

## Reproduce

Run from the workspace root, with one BLAS thread:

```
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u tonight/operator/adversarial3d/search.py --nodes 28 --starts 24 --refine-nodes 56 --refine-top 4 --maxiter 350 --checks 80 112 144

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u tonight/operator/adversarial3d/search.py --nodes 36 --starts 60 --diverse --refine-nodes 72 --refine-top 6 --maxiter 400 --checks 96 128 160 --output diverse_results.json

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u tonight/operator/adversarial3d/verify_streamed.py

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u tonight/operator/adversarial3d/verify_streamed.py --input diverse_results.json --output diverse_verification.json --replicates 4

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u tonight/operator/adversarial3d/check_all_coarse.py
```

`results.json` and `diverse_results.json` contain every optimization.
`all_coarse_crosscheck.json` records all 168 finer-grid checks and the final
GH96 refinements.  `verification.json` and `diverse_verification.json` contain
separate streamed and randomized checks.  `SUMMARY.json` is a compact index;
`SHA256.json` freezes the complete screen.
