# Directed R3 profile certificate

This package checks a *local scalar profile*, not the global Grothendieck
operator norm.  A global result also needs an outer cover of every admissible
amplitude, transverse cubic norm and cumulant.  All fit files remain numerical
discovery records.  The `certify_...` scripts accept boxes only through exact
rational algebra and directed 256-bit MPFR arithmetic.

The fitting operator is (P_1-bP_3).  The main frozen profile has (b=18/25),
amplitude (u=.755), transverse cubic norm (r_0=.25755), and cumulant


\[
\kappa_0=.53,\qquad \theta_0=\frac{\kappa_0}{3(\kappa_0+2)}=\frac{53}{759}.
\]

`freeze` rounds coefficients to explicit rational numbers, replaces the even
matrix by the exact rank-one matrix (E=pp^T), and verifies every relevant PSD
gate exactly.  The odd matrix receives an explicit rational diagonal repair.
The frozen profile, rather than the floating fit, is the certified object.

Use raw Hermites (H_2(z)=z^2-1), (H_3(z)=z^3-3z), (p=(p_0,p_2)),
and (L(z)=\ell z+\beta H_3(z)).  Set

\[
S(q)=q^3-3q,\quad g(q)=c q+d_0S(q),\quad
\rho=1+\zeta^2/G,\quad a(q)=\zeta[-S(q)+g(q)/(2G)].
\]

The local potential is the supremum over ((s,m_1,m_3)) of

\[
2\mathbb E_Z\max\{|L(Z)|,|B(q,Z)|\}
-\rho s^2-R_{11}m_1^2-2R_{13}m_1m_3-R_{33}m_3^2
+g(q)^2/(4G),
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
B(q,z)={}&q\gamma+q(y-p_0p_2r_0)H_2(z)
 +[a(q)+\rho s][p_0+p_2H_2(z)]\\
&+(R_{11}m_1+R_{13}m_3)z
 +(R_{13}m_1+R_{33}m_3)H_3(z).
\end{aligned}
\]

The physical dual moments lie in

\[
s\in[-1,1],\quad m_1\in[-1,1],\quad m_3\in[-5/2,5/2].
\]

Indeed (p_0^2+2p_2^2<1), and the raw Hermite norms are (1,\sqrt2,\sqrt6).
Restricting to this box loses no maximum: interchange the support maximum and
the PSD quadratic completion, whose maximizer can always be chosen to be the
physical moment vector itself.  Reflection makes the resulting supremum even
in (q), so the cover only needs (q\ge0).

The scalar majorant is

\[
M(q)=q_0+q_2q^2+q_4q^4+q_6q^6+q\varphi(q)
 -[1+\theta_0(q^2-1)]\varphi'(q)-\Psi(q),
\]

where (arphi) is a rational Chebyshev expansion,

\[
\varphi(q)=\frac{q}{1+q^2}
\sum_j a_jT_j\!\left(\frac{q^2-1}{q^2+1}\right),
\]

and, writing (w=\varphi'(q)-\gamma_0-\gamma_2(q^2-1)) and


\[
\lambda(q)=-1-\theta_0(q^2-1),
\]


\[
\Psi(q)=
\begin{cases}
w^2/(2\eta),&w\ge\eta\lambda(q),\\
\lambda(q)w-\eta\lambda(q)^2/2,&w<\eta\lambda(q).
\end{cases}
\]

The directed cover adds the exact reserve (10^{-6}(1+q^6)) to (M).

## Every box covers its entire interior

For the three moment coordinates, product corner interpolation bounds the
concave quadratic error by

\[
\frac14\{\rho\,\Delta s^2+R_{11}\Delta m_1^2+R_{33}\Delta m_3^2\}.
\]

The support function is convex in its polynomial argument.  On (q\in[a,b]),
the defect of the cubic (B_0(q,z)) from its endpoint chord is exactly a cubic
coefficient times (-(q-a)(b-q)(q+a+b)).  The code uses

\[
\mathbb E|Z^2-1|=4\phi(1)<1
\]

and bounds this support-function defect by

\[
\frac32 b\,\left|\zeta\left(-1+\frac{d_0}{2G}\right)\right|
(|p_0|+|p_2|)\,(b-a)^2.
\]

Finally let (f(q)=g(q)^2/(4G)-M(q)).  Directed Taylor jets compute an
interval lower bound on (f'').  If (f''\ge-K), its defect from the endpoint
chord is at most (K(b-a)^2/8).  The clipped Young function is (C^1);
both branch second derivatives are bounded, and their union is used whenever
the interval does not decide the active branch.  Therefore the maximum corner
residual plus these three exact allowances bounds the entire four-dimensional
box.  No numerical maximizer, finite sample, or floating root is used to accept
a box.

`gaussian_max_certificate.py` uses floating roots only to propose rational
polynomial surrogates.  Their coefficient discrepancy is paid globally by an
exact (L^2) allowance.  All Gaussian primitive values and endpoint integrals
are outward MPFR intervals.

## Exact whole tail

For (q\ge64), triangle inequalities and the PSD completion give

\[
\Phi(q)\le2\mathbb E|L|+2\mathbb E|B_0(q,Z)|+
\rho(p_0^2+2p_2^2)+R_{11}+6R_{33}+g(q)^2/(4G).
\]

The rational Chebyshev bounds (|T_j|\le1), (|T'_j|\le j^2) control the
majorant tail.  The resulting degree-six difference polynomial has all
nonnegative coefficients after the exact substitution (q=64+t), (t\ge0).
`directed_local_tail.json` records those coefficients.

## Outer transport

Freeze the scalar centering (	heta_0) throughout an outer cumulant interval.
At an actual cumulant (kappa), the centered Stein variable has variance

\[
V-2\theta_0\kappa/3+\theta_0^2(\kappa+2),
\]

and nuisance covariance

\[
\mathbb E[U_{\theta_0}(Q^2-1)]
=\kappa/3-\theta_0(\kappa+2).
\]

The outer price includes this covariance with the **positive** coefficient

\[
+\gamma_2[\kappa/3-\theta_0(\kappa+2)].
\]

Choose (y(r)=y_0+p_0p_2(r-r_0)).  Then the complete local potential stays
identical for every (r), while the outer quadratic becomes

\[
b r^2-2[\gamma-p_0(y_0/p_2-p_0r_0)]r+(y_0/p_2-p_0r_0)^2.
\]

`price_frozen_profile.py` evaluates all endpoint vertices by directed
arithmetic.  The cumulant and tensor-shape endpoint reduction uses the exact
signs (q_6+\zeta^2\ge0), (G-d_0-\zeta^2\ge0) and the fresh633 moment
bound.  This outer file remains explicitly conditional until every compact
q-region and the tail have passed.

```sh
python3 certificate/certify_local_region.py results/shape_residual_n16_clip1_res1_u0.755_r0.25755_k0.53_pub0_pe0.15.json --qa 0 --qb 1 --reserve .000001 --max-nodes 100000 --output results/directed_region_0_1.json.gz
```

An unfinished compact cover is marked `INCOMPLETE_NO_CERTIFICATE` and can be
resumed with `--resume` pointing to its exact-profile checkpoint.  Dependencies
are Python3, NumPy and the system MPFR shared library.
