\[
\boxed{\mathbb E|Q|<847/1000=.847,\qquad

## Proof

Write `kappa=E Q^4-3`, `U=||DQ||^2/3-1`, and parameterize the coupled shape by

\[
\mathbb E U^2=\kappa(7/45+s/15),\qquad 0\le\kappa\le90,
\quad0\le s\le1.
\]

The scalar price of any complete-halfline Stein row with frozen rational data `(q0,q2,q4,q6,eta)` is

\[
L(\kappa,s)=c+q_0+q_2+(3+\kappa)q_4
+[15+15\kappa+(K-633s/15)\kappa^{3/2}]q_6
+\eta\kappa(7/90+s/30),
\]

where `c=sqrt(2/pi)` and `K=194/sqrt(10)`. The complete scalar comparison and Stein identity imply `E|Q|<=L(kappa,s)` for every normalized cubic. In particular, a row is valid outside its original discovery or pricing interval. Every row here has `q6>0` and `eta>0`; its price is affine in `s` and convex in `kappa`, because `K-633/15>0`.
