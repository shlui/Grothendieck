# Independent cubic-head polynomial source audit

The all-halfline majorant and physical source factors in
`tonight/moments/CUBIC_HEAD_SOURCE_GATE.md` pass independent review.

The monotone cubic is `L(z)=Az+Bz³`, with `A>0,B≥0`. In normalized
Hermites it is `(A+3B)z + sqrt(6)B H3(z)`, so the source pairing is
exactly `(A+3B)u + sqrt(6)B v + r`. The transverse normalized cubic is
independent of the longitudinal Gaussian and its pairing with `k` is
exactly `r`. These give the displayed support comparison from the
pointwise diamond constraint.

For `q=w(A+Bw)²`, `dq/dw=(A+Bw)(A+3Bw)`; no factor two is missing.
Differentiating `F(q)=c exp(−w/2)/(A+3Bw)` gives the stated alternating
derivative recurrence. I independently reconstructed its multivariate
polynomials using only dictionaries of Python `Fraction` coefficients.
The first three numerators have respectively 5, 12 and 22 strictly
positive nonzero coefficients. Thus `F'''≤0` for every `q≥0`, uniformly
over the full stated parameter range, including `B=0`.

Taylor's quadratic majorant for `F` is integrated as
`J(x)=J(0)+integral_0^x (x−t)F(t²)dt`. Its three integration factors are
`1/2,1/12,1/30`; with the Taylor coefficient `F''(0)/2`, they produce
exactly the displayed sextic coefficient. Also
`J(0)=E|AZ+BZ³|=c(A+2B)`. This proves the bound globally rather than on
sampled source values.

The negative quartic coefficient multiplies the **exact** fourth moment
`3+κ`; no upper fourth-moment substitution is made. The sextic coefficient
is positive, so the coupled 633 sixth-moment upper bound is substituted
in the safe direction. Its shape variable is the same `V` as in every
other price for the transverse source.

The independent checker
`check_cubic_head_polynomial.py` imports neither the producer's polynomial
engine nor its Arb implementation. It also reconstructs the rational
example coefficients and uses 256-bit directed MPFR intervals for the
two moment prices. At `A=97/40,B=1/6,κ=53/100,u=151/200,v=−1/4`, both
claimed strict rational bounds pass:

* `r < 252844293/10^9` at `V=7κ/45`;
* `r < 250945617/10^9` at `V=2κ/9`.

Output is retained in `cubic_head_polynomial_independent.json`. The factor
`d=sqrt(6)/6` and its multiplication by the negative example `v` have
the correct signs. The full price is affine in the physical shape.

The example value `v=−1/4` is not a restriction on arbitrary operator
sign pairs. A global norm proof must keep or rigorously eliminate the
actual longitudinal cubic moment. The note states that limitation
correctly. For zero source, it correctly avoids assigning an artificial
normalized cubic or cumulant. No substantive proof gap was found.
