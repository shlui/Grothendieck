# Odd-tail operator screen

This folder screens
\[
 S_{b,t}=P_1-bP_3+t\Pi_{\rm odd},\qquad
 \Pi_{\rm odd}f(x)=\frac{f(x)-f(-x)}2.
\]
Here \(\Pi_{\rm odd}\) contains **all odd Hermite levels**, including levels 1 and 3. Thus the actual multipliers are \(1+t\) on level 1, \(-b+t\) on level 3, and \(t\) on every higher odd level. This is not a correction only to the tail above level 3.

For \(b\in[.70,.84],t\in[-.02,.04]\), the largest absolute multiplier is \(1+t\). The vector-valued bilinear norm is therefore at most \(1+t\) by the Hilbert-space operator bound. For the odd radial unit field \(F_d(x)=x/|x|\), its level-one energy tends to 1 and level-three energy tends to 0, while \(\Pi_{\rm odd}F_d=F_d\). Its vector numerator tends to \(1+t\). Hence this is the exact asymptotic vector numerator and norm scale for the sequence.

For an antipodally paired Gaussian partition, fold each pair into one representative. Let \(p_i\) be the total Gaussian probability of that pair and \(F_\ell(i,\alpha)\) be twice the integral of normalized odd Hermite feature \(\alpha\) over its representative cell. The folded matrix is
\[
 A=F_1F_1^T-bF_3F_3^T+t\operatorname{diag}(p).
\]
The diagonal represents \(t\Pi_{\rm odd}\) on odd cell functions. It is not an unweighted identity matrix.

## Explicit scalar witnesses

Let \(Y_n\) be a normalized diffuse third-chaos sum, independent of \(Z\), converging to a standard Gaussian \(Y\). For an odd threshold function \(q\), use
\[
 f=\operatorname{sign}(q(Z)+Y),\qquad
 g=\operatorname{sign}(q(Z)-Y).
\]
The CLT-limit third-chaos projections consist of the longitudinal \(H_3(Z)\) coefficient and the first Gaussian coefficient of the diffuse cubic coordinate. Write
\[
 u=\mathbb E[Z\operatorname{erf}(q(Z)/\sqrt2)],\quad
 v=\mathbb E[H_3(Z)\operatorname{erf}(q(Z)/\sqrt2)],\quad
 r=\sqrt{2/\pi}\mathbb E e^{-q(Z)^2/2}.
\]
Then the witness value is
\[
 C_{b,t}(q)=u^2-bv^2+br^2+t m,\qquad
 m=2\mathbb E|\operatorname{erf}(q(Z)/\sqrt2)|-1.
\]
These are **lower tests for the scalar norm**. Therefore \((1+t)/C_{b,t}(q)\) is a method ceiling, not a proved Grothendieck lower bound. A hemisphere equal-sign witness gives \((2/\pi)(1-b/6)+t\). The screen also includes diffuse and coordinate cubic anti-sign witnesses.

For a positive odd threshold on \(Z>0\), stationarity gives
\[
 q(z)=\frac{u z-bvH_3(z)+t\operatorname{sign}z}{br}.
\]
In particular, the odd constant is \(q_0=t/(br)\), with the \(r\) defined above. Omitting this direction produces a weaker adversarial test and an overly optimistic ceiling.

## Numerical result

The witness-generation minimax screen covers \(b\in[.70,.84]\), \(t\in[-.02,.04]\), including negative corrections. A subsequent stationary polish gives approximately
\[
 b=.74277462646,\qquad t=.01104909460,
 \qquad \frac{1+t}{C_{b,t}}=1.77563796633.
\]
The normalized witness norm is approximately 0.563177865623, leaving only 0.000202416067 below the target cap \(40/71\). Thus this family provides a modest increase over the pure \(b=.72\) witness margin, not the desired 0.001 margin.

The polished threshold is
\[
 q(z)=.05491738185\operatorname{sign}z+2.50523013064z+z^3/\sqrt6.
\]
At the interior stationary point, \(r=-v\) and \(m=u^2\). Symmetric quintic and asymmetric cubic/quintic probes at the nearby minimax point gave essentially the same scalar value. Held-out Gaussian quadrature agrees near machine precision. These checks assess the selected witness family only; they do not rule out stronger all-sign witnesses.

Run `python screen_odd_tail.py` for the full witness-generation history and `python polish_stationary.py` for the interior stationary refinement. Results are `odd_tail_screen.json` and `stationary_polish.json`. No directed global operator-norm certificate is asserted.

For actual signs with half-sum \(h\) and half-difference \(k\), the exact new scalar term is \(t(1-2\mathbb E k^2)\). A global fiber proof must retain this support-mass correction; adding \(t\) as a free constant discards it.
