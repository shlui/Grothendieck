# All-active tail domain repair

The unbounded-tail algebra in `certify_continuous_profile_scalars.py` assumes that every target and regression hinge is active. An independent review identified that the first high-amplitude rows had target hinges beyond the initial candidate cutoff64. Those rows' original tail acceptance was therefore provisional.

The verifier now computes the exact largest breakpoint and rejects every candidate cutoff below it. Accepted tails explicitly record `last_hinge` and `all_hinges_active`. All12 high-amplitude rows were freshly regenerated: rows0--2 now start their tails at128, row7 at256, and the others at64. Their compact portions were extended to those exact cutoffs. All12 pass, with3,862 compact Taylor leaves; their expectation prices are unchanged.

The earlier broad profiles already had all hinges active before their saved cutoffs, so the missing explicit guard did not invalidate their scalar inequalities. Every future portable replay must carry the guard (or independently assert the same domain condition).
