# An analytic source gate retaining the longitudinal cubic moment

This is a new physical source constraint for a cubic operator norm proof. It supplies no complete operator norm endpoint by itself. Its all-halfline scalar inequality is proved analytically and checked by exact rational polynomial arithmetic. The moment input is the included freshly proved coefficient-633 theorem in `reduced_search/energy/633/PROOF.md`; no sampled source law or numerical Gaussian quadrature enters the gate.

Let \(c=\sqrt{2/\pi}\), let \(Z\) be a standard Gaussian independent of \(W\), and use normalized \(H_3(z)=(z^3-3z)/\sqrt6\). Suppose \(|h|+|k|\le1\), and write
\[
 u=\mathbb E[Zh],\qquad v=\mathbb E[H_3(Z)h],\qquad
 r=\|P_3^W\mathbb E_Z k\|_2.
\]
For \(r>0\), take \(Q=P_3^W\mathbb E_Zk/r\), a normalized third Gaussian chaos in \(W\). Its fourth cumulant and Malliavin shape are
\[
 \kappa=\mathbb E Q^4-3,\qquad V=\mathbb E[(\|DQ\|^2/3-1)^2].
\]
In particular, \(V\) is the same physical shape that must occur in any other scalar price for this source \(Q\).

## The all-halfline scalar inequality

Fix \(A>0,B\ge0\), and put
\[
 L(z)=Az+Bz^3=a z+dH_3(z),\qquad a=A+3B,\quad d=\sqrt6B,
 \qquad J_{A,B}(x)=\mathbb E_Z\max\{|L(Z)|,|x|\}.
\]
For every real \(x\),
\[
\boxed{
 J_{A,B}(x)\le c\left[A+2B+\frac{x^2}{2A}
 -\frac{A+6B}{24A^4}x^4
 +\frac{A^2+20AB+120B^2}{240A^7}x^6\right].
}
\]

**Proof.** The polynomial \(L\) is odd and strictly increasing. For \(x\ge0\), write \(x=L(z)\), \(z\ge0\). Differentiating the distribution-function expression for \(J\) gives
\[
 J'(x)=\Pr\{|Z|\le z\},\qquad
 J''(x)=\frac{c e^{-z^2/2}}{A+3Bz^2}.
\]
Also \(J(0)=c(A+2B)\) and \(J'(0)=0\). Put \(w=z^2,q=x^2=w(A+Bw)^2\), and define \(F(q)=J''(\sqrt q)\). Then
\[
 F(q)=\frac{c e^{-w/2}}{A+3Bw},\qquad
 \frac{d}{dq}=\frac{1}{(A+Bw)(A+3Bw)}\frac{d}{dw}.
\]
To verify \(F'''(q)\le0\) on the whole halfline, set \(D_1=A+Bw,D_3=A+3Bw\). Start with \(P_0=1,r_0=0,s_0=1\), and successively define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P_{j+1}&=(P_j/2-\partial_wP_j)D_1D_3
             +r_jBP_jD_3+3s_jBP_jD_1,\\
 r_{j+1}&=r_j+2,\qquad s_{j+1}=s_j+2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Direct differentiation proves
\[
 (-1)^j F^{(j)}(q)=
 \frac{c e^{-w/2}P_j(A,B,w)}{D_1^{r_j}D_3^{s_j}}.
\]
The three exact polynomials \(P_1,P_2,P_3\) have respectively 5,12,22 coefficients, all nonnegative. They are printed in the certificate JSON. This is a finite rational-algebra certificate for \(F'''\le0\) on every \(q\ge0\), for all \(A>0,B\ge0\). Consequently Taylor's formula with its integral remainder gives
\[
 F(q)\le F(0)+F'(0)q+\tfrac12F''(0)q^2,
\]
where
\[
 F(0)=\frac cA,\quad F'(0)=-\frac{c(A+6B)}{2A^4},\quad
 F''(0)=\frac{c(A^2+20AB+120B^2)}{4A^7}.
\]
Integrating twice with respect to \(x\), from \(J''(x)=F(x^2)\), proves the displayed degree-six majorant. Evenness supplies \(x<0\). This proof also covers \(B=0\).

## Physical source constraint and exact moments

Since \(|h|+|k|\le1\), pointwise
\[
 L(Z)h+Qk\le\max\{|L(Z)|,|Q|\}.
\]
Taking expectations, using independence of \(Z,W\), and the definition of \(Q\), yields
\[
 au+dv+r\le\mathbb E J_{A,B}(Q).
\]
Thus, if \(q_0,q_2,q_4,q_6\) are the coefficients inside the square brackets of the scalar majorant, then the checked moment theorem implies
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
 au+dv+r\le \mathcal A_{A,B}(\kappa,V)
 =c\bigg[q_0+q_2+(3+\kappa)q_4
 +q_6\bigg(15+15\kappa+\frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}\kappa^{3/2}
       -633\sqrt\kappa\left(V-\frac{7\kappa}{45}\right)\bigg)\bigg].
\end{aligned}}
\]
Here \(q_6>0\), \(0\le\kappa\le90\), and \(7\kappa/45\le V\le2\kappa/9\). The fourth moment is exact; its negative coefficient causes no inequality reversal. For \(r=0\), the normalized source is undefined, and the separate zero-source case must be used rather than assigning an artificial \(\kappa\).

The gate retains the longitudinal cubic moment \(v\). Eliminating \(v\) before using this constraint discards its principal improvement.

Take
\[
 A=\frac{97}{40},\quad B=\frac16,
 \quad a=\frac{117}{40},\quad d=\frac{\sqrt6}{6}.
\]
The exact coefficients are
\[
 (q_0,q_2,q_4,q_6)=
 \left(\frac{331}{120},\frac{20}{97},
 -\frac{1096000}{265587843},
 \frac{106274560000}{727184560303017}\right).
\]
At \(\kappa=53/100,u=151/200,v=-1/4\), parameterize
\(V=\kappa(7/45+\zeta/15)\), \(0\le\zeta\le1\). Directed 512-bit evaluations of the exact moment prices give

The full price is affine in \(\zeta\), so the same affine interpolation applies between these endpoints. The \(v=-1/4\) values illustrate the constraint; a global operator norm proof must retain and quantify the actual \(v\), rather than assume this value.
