# Cubic source caps retaining the shared Malliavin shape

This note proves a physical source constraint for the unresolved middle-amplitude cubic operator calculation. It is not a full operator norm bound or a new Grothendieck endpoint. The scalar coefficients were recovered from earlier discovery data; the whole-line directed checks have been run freshly here. The moment input is the included fresh coefficient-633 theorem, proved and checked in `reduced_search/energy/633/PROOF.md`.

Let \(c=\sqrt{2/\pi}\). For a normalized third Gaussian chaos \(Q\), put
\[
 \kappa=\mathbb E Q^4-3,\quad
 \tau=\tfrac13\|DQ\|^2,\quad V=\mathbb E(\tau-1)^2.
\]
The fixed-chaos integration-by-parts identity is
\(\mathbb E[Qg(Q)]=\mathbb E[\tau g'(Q)]\).
The checked moment theorem supplies
\[
0\le\kappa\le90,\qquad \frac{7\kappa}{45}\le V\le\frac{2\kappa}{9},
\qquad
\mathbb E Q^6\le15+15\kappa+\frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}\kappa^{3/2}
 -633\sqrt\kappa\left(V-\frac{7\kappa}{45}\right).
\]
The source and native scalar profile must use the same \(V\). Maximizing the source price over \(V\) first loses this constraint.

## Physical source inequality

Let \(Z\) be a standard Gaussian independent of the transverse Gaussian vector \(W\). Let \(|h|+|k|\le1\), put \(u=\mathbb E[Zh]\ge0\), and suppose
\[
 r=\|P_3^W\mathbb E_Zk\|_2>0,
 \qquad Q(W)=P_3^W\mathbb E_Zk/r.
\]
For every \(a>0\), pointwise
\(aZh+Qk\le\max\{a|Z|,|Q|\}\). Therefore
\[
 au+r\le\mathbb E J_a(Q),\qquad
 J_a(x)=\mathbb E_Z\max\{a|Z|,|x|\}
 =ac e^{-x^2/(2a^2)}+|x|\operatorname{erf}\frac{|x|}{\sqrt2a}.
\]
In fact \(J_a(x)=\mathbb E_Z|x+aZ|\), so for a second independent standard Gaussian \(X\),
\(\mathbb E J_a(X)=c\sqrt{1+a^2}\).
The inequality requires no sampled law or independence between \(Q\) and \(k\).

## Scalar Stein majorant

Write \(R(x)=e^{x^2/2}\int_x^\infty e^{-t^2/2}\,dt\), \(G(x)=1-xR(x)\), and \(s=\sqrt{1+a^2}\). The odd Gaussian Stein solution of
\[
 f_a'(x)-xf_a(x)=J_a(x)-cs
\]
has, for \(x\ge0\), derivative
\[
 f_a'(x)=-csG(x)+ca e^{-x^2/(2a^2)}G(sx/a).
\]
This follows by integrating the explicit formula for \(J_a\) against \(e^{-x^2/2}\). Its sign and tail bounds are
\[
 -\frac{cs}{x^2}\le f_a'(x)\le0\qquad(x>0).
\]
The lower bound follows directly from the displayed derivative, \(G\ge0\), and \(G(x)\le x^{-2}\). For the upper bound, use the standard Ornstein–Uhlenbeck representation of the Stein solution:
\(f_a'(x)=-\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}\mathbb E[J_a''(e^{-t}x+\sqrt{1-e^{-2t}}X)]\,dt\le0\), since \(J_a''\ge0\).

For \(U=\tau-1\ge-1\), define
\[
 \Psi_\eta(t)=\begin{cases}t^2/(2\eta),&t\le\eta,\\t-\eta/2,&t>\eta.\end{cases}
\]
It is exactly \(\sup_{v\ge-1}\{-vt-\eta v^2/2\}\). For any admissible odd \(C^1\) test \(\phi\), Stein's identity gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E J_a(Q)
 &=cs-\mathbb E[U f_a'(Q)]\\
 &\le cs+\mathbb E\!\left[\phi'(Q)-Q\phi(Q)
       +\Psi_\eta(f_a'(Q)-\phi'(Q))\right]+\frac\eta2V.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus a complete-real-line scalar majorant
\[
\phi'(x)-x\phi(x)+\Psi_\eta(f_a'(x)-\phi'(x))
 \le q_0+q_2x^2+q_4x^4+q_6x^6,\qquad q_6\ge0,
\]
yields the **shape-dependent** source cap
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
 A_a(\kappa,V)={}&cs+q_0+q_2+(3+\kappa)q_4\\
 &+\left[15+15\kappa+\frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}\kappa^{3/2}
       +\frac{633\cdot7}{45}\kappa^{3/2}\right]q_6
       +\left(\frac\eta2-633\sqrt\kappa\,q_6\right)V,\\
 r\le{}&A_a(\kappa,V)-au.
\end{aligned}}
\]
Every frozen scalar row is valid for all \(\kappa\); the listed \(\kappa=.53\) is a fitting point, not a restriction of the scalar majorant.
