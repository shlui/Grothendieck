# Exact rational derivatives and physical-center local covers

For a frozen coefficient vector of length m, set

\[
\varphi(q)=\frac{q}{1+q^2}\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}c_j
T_j\left(\frac{q^2-1}{q^2+1}\right).
\]

Define A0=1, A1=q²−1 and
Aj=2(q²−1)Aj−1−(q²+1)²Aj−2. Then
Tj((q²−1)/(q²+1))=Aj/(1+q²)^j. Consequently
φ=N0/(1+q²)^m with an explicit rational polynomial N0 of degree at most
2m−1. Its derivative numerators obey

\[
 N_{k+1}=(1+q^2)N_k'-2(m+k)qN_k,
 \qquad \varphi^{(k)}=\frac{N_k}{(1+q^2)^{m+k}}.
\]

On a rational interval [a,b]⊂[0,∞), exact Bernstein coefficients bound Nk
between their smallest and largest coefficients. The denominator is positive
and lies in [(1+a²)^(m+k),(1+b²)^(m+k)]. Directed interval division therefore
gives rigorous bounds for every derivative. The scalar residual and clipped
Fenchel-price derivative formulas are unchanged from the audited Jet method.

The integer Horner implementation forms Bernstein coefficients without
Fraction arithmetic in the inner loop. Let polynomial coefficients have common
denominator S, and a=A/d,b=B/d. At degree j, store Bernstein coefficients with
denominator S*d^j*j!. Multiplication by q=a(1−t)+bt and addition of the next
Horner constant ci gives exactly

\[
 u_k^{(j)}=A(j-k)u_k^{(j-1)}+Bk u_{k-1}^{(j-1)}
                +c_i^{\rm int}d^j j!.
\]

Missing endpoint coefficients are zero. This proves the integer recurrence
and its exact equality with the usual Bernstein transformation. The checker
`audit_rational_phi_bounds.py` verifies all four derivative identities by 35
exact rational zeros after clearing a common denominator: the difference
numerator has degree at most 34. It also compares 92 exact Bernstein ranges
against a separate direct affine power transformation. All checks pass.

For the physical-center cover, let m=(s,m1,m3) be the actual conditional moment
vector, N=diag(ρ,R)⪰0, and c be a rational center. Use the three local basis
functions H=(p0+p2*(Z²−1),Z,Z³−3Z). The physical local objective
J has the exact decomposition

\[
 J=2\mathbb E[Lh+(B_0+Nc\cdot H)k]-c^TNc+
       (m-c)^TN(m-c)+g(q)^2/(4G).
\]

If m is in the rectangular box centered at c with radii (rs,r1,r3), its last
quadratic term is at most

\[
 \rho r_s^2+R_{11}r_1^2+2|R_{13}|r_1r_3+R_{33}r_3^2.
\]

The first terms, maximized over the four pointwise ternary choices, are exactly
the previously audited center value. Thus center value plus this radius bounds
**original physical assignments with moments in the box**. It does not assume
that the dual potential is bounded by its center value. Endpoint interpolation
in q, with the exact scalar semiconvexity and cubic-source allowance, covers
the entire q interval.

Bessel's inequality places every physical vector in
s²/(p0²+2p2²)+m1²+m3²/6≤1. A moment box whose closest point to the origin in
this diagonal quadratic metric has value greater than 1 contains no physical
moment and may be discarded. Gaussian Z-reflection changes the sign of both
odd moments and preserves the objective, so m1≥0 is sufficient. The recorded
completed [0,8] tree happened to need no ellipsoid exclusions; its reflection
restriction and physical-center radius still apply.

All these statements are exact mathematical bounds. A completed local cover
proves a scalar majorant at one frozen profile. A global operator result still
requires the full outer parameter domain to be covered.
