# Exact reflected witness for b=7171/10000

Define

\[
N(b)=\sup_{d\ge1}\sup_{|f|,|g|\le1}
 \langle(P_1-bP_3)f,g\rangle_{L^2(\gamma_d)}.
\]

For the exact rational threshold

\[
q(z)=az+cz^3,\qquad
a=\frac{26412631}{10^7},\quad c=\frac{4084047}{10^7},
\qquad b=\frac{7171}{10000},
\]

the directed certificate proves

\[
N(b)\ge W>\frac{56323352479769}{10^{14}}
 =0.56323352479769.
\]

The enclosed limiting witness is

\[
0.56323352479769013377939128145064207\ldots
\le W\le
0.56323352479769013443552131880652919\ldots.
\]

Consequently the asymptotically unit-numerator reciprocal-norm construction
has ceiling

\[
\frac1{N(b)}\le\frac1W
 <\frac{1775462496412}{10^{12}}
 =1.775462496412.
\]

The sharper directed upper endpoint for `1/W` is
`1.77546249641157915826962638014795618...`.  This is a ceiling for the fixed
operator construction, **not an achieved Grothendieck lower endpoint** and
not an upper bound on the real Grothendieck constant.  The witness is below
`40/71` by at least `0.0001467568924507106349012164047384...`, so it does not
exclude a `1.775` lower endpoint from a future global norm upper certificate.

## Actual original-degree sign functions

All Gaussian variables below are independent.  Use normalized probabilists'
Hermite functions, with `H3(x)=(x^3-3x)/sqrt(6)`, and set

\[
Y_n=n^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^nH_3(X_j),\qquad
f_n=\operatorname{sgn}(Y_n+q(Z)),\qquad
g_n=\operatorname{sgn}(-Y_n+q(Z)).
\]

They are bounded signs in the actual original Gaussian space of dimension
`n+1`.  The ordinary CLT gives `Y_n => Y`, with `Y` standard normal.  It is
essential that the retained transverse channel has **original degree three**.
Distribution convergence alone does not justify convergence of the degree-one
and degree-three projection pairings.

The complete original-degree accounting, proved for this construction in
Appendix `Original-degree accounting for the sign witness` of the main
manuscript and in `../cubic720/README.md`, applies unchanged to this threshold.
The required monotonicity is `q'(z)=a+3cz^2 >= a>0`.  A concise proof is as
follows.  First replace each sign by a bounded smooth approximation with
bounded derivatives in its scalar argument.  Expand separately in each
summand `H3(X_j)/sqrt(n)`.  Hermite orthogonality and Gaussian moments give the
following coefficient orders for distinct transverse indices `i,j,k`:

| Original Hermite basis term | Coefficient size | Total squared-energy size |
|---|---|---|
| H1(Z) | tends to m1 | retained |
| H1(Xi) | O(n^(-3/2)) | O(n^(-2)) |
| H3(Z) | tends to m3 | retained |
| H2(Z)H1(Xi) | O(n^(-3/2)) | O(n^(-2)) |
| H1(Z)H2(Xi) | O(n^(-1)) | O(n^(-1)) |
| H1(Z)H1(Xi)H1(Xj) | O(n^(-3)) | O(n^(-4)) |
| H3(Xi) | n^(-1/2)mY+o(n^(-1/2)) | tends to mY^2 |
| H2(Xi)H1(Xj) | O(n^(-5/2)) | O(n^(-3)) |
| H1(Xi)H1(Xj)H1(Xk) | O(n^(-9/2)) | O(n^(-6)) |

These exhaust original total degrees one and three.  The smooth pure-Z
coefficients converge by the CLT; the surviving transverse coefficients
converge to Gaussian derivative moments, equivalently `E[Yf]`.  For the
reflected `g_n`, pure-Z coefficients have the same limit, and transverse
cubic coefficients have the opposite limit.

Finally the smoothing can be removed uniformly in `n`: conditioning on
`Y_n`, monotonicity of `q` gives

\[
\Pr(|Y_n+q(Z)|\le\delta\mid Y_n)
 \le \frac{2\delta}{a\sqrt{2\pi}}.
\]

Orthogonal projection is an `L2` contraction.  Thus the uniform sign-smoothing
error controls both projection norms and cross pairings independently of the
dimension.  It follows that

\[
\lim_n\langle P_1f_n,g_n\rangle=m_1^2,\qquad
\lim_n\langle P_3f_n,g_n\rangle=m_3^2-m_Y^2.
\]

Since every finite-dimensional pairing is bounded above by `N(b)`,

\[
N(b)\ge W=m_1^2-b(m_3^2-m_Y^2).
\]

## Exact integral formulas and enclosure

Analytic integration in the independent limit variable `Y` gives
`E_Y sign(Y+q(z))=erf(q(z)/sqrt(2))`.  Gaussian integration by parts then gives

\[
m_Y=\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty E(z)\,dz,\quad
m_1=\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty q'(z)E(z)\,dz,\quad
m_3=\frac2{\pi\sqrt6}\int_0^\infty(z^2-1)q'(z)E(z)\,dz,
\]

where `E(z)=exp(-(z^2+q(z)^2)/2)`.  In particular `m3` is signed; the producer
does not replace its compact integral by an absolute integral.

The producer partitions `[0,5]` into 320 exact rational cells.  It computes
Taylor coefficients through degree ten at each midpoint, integrates even
terms through degree eight exactly, and bounds the degree-ten remainder on
the entire cell using outward MPFR intervals.  The jets represent derivatives
divided by factorial.  For any integrand `F` and cell center `z0` and radius
`h`, the enclosed integral is

\[
\sum_{j=0,2,\ldots,8}\frac{F^{(j)}(z_0)}{j!}
 \frac{2h^{j+1}}{j+1}
 +\frac{F^{(10)}([z_0-h,z_0+h])}{10!}
 \frac{2h^{11}}{11}.
\]

For the tail `z>=L=5`, put `c0=1+a^2`.  Because `q(z)>=az`,

\[
E(z)\le e^{-c_0z^2/2},\quad
J_0\le\frac{e^{-c_0L^2/2}}{c_0L},\quad
J_2\le\frac{Le^{-c_0L^2/2}+J_0}{c_0},\quad
J_4\le\frac{L^3e^{-c_0L^2/2}+3J_2}{c_0}.
\]

The three omitted moments are bounded respectively by

\[
\frac2\pi J_0,\qquad
\frac2\pi(aJ_0+3cJ_2),\qquad
\frac2{\pi\sqrt6}\bigl(a(J_2+J_0)+3c(J_4+J_2)\bigr).
\]

All tail integrands are nonnegative because `L>1`, so each tail is added with
zero lower endpoint and the explicit upper endpoint.  Their upper bounds are
less than `8e-46`, `2.7e-44` and `2.9e-43`.  Directed evaluation at 384 bits gives
the recorded witness interval of width less than `6.6e-19`; no floating
quadrature convergence is used.

The complete result, moment intervals, input rationals, MPFR self-test and
producer hash are in `cubic7171_norm_witness.json`.  The unchanged `.72`
producer is recorded as an external dependency; it is not modified by this
package.

## Independent acceptance

A distinct checker, `tonight/audit/replay_cubic7171_witness.py`, recomputed
all three signed integrals using Arb at 512 bits, Taylor order twelve and 400
rational cells.  It separately implemented the Gaussian-tail recurrence and
imported neither the producer nor its MPFR adapter.  Its accepted output,
copied as `independent_replay.json`, has narrower intervals agreeing with all
producer moments and the resulting witness and reciprocal intervals.  The
producer JSON hash checked by that replay is
`04c25cce0c7c5b5439dd94d9c9707aa7dafced367359e11b2778f808913adff4`.
Exact Fraction comparisons verify that its witness lower endpoint exceeds
`56323352479769/10^14` and its reciprocal upper endpoint is below
`1775462496412/10^12`.  Thus the clean theorem is independently accepted,
without any historical global scalar norm premise.
