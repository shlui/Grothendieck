\[
\kappa_6\le \frac{194}{\sqrt{10}}\kappa^{3/2}

## Analytic inputs checked

I independently checked the tensor reduction from the retained continuation report, including its normalization and the following identities and inequalities.

* With \(6\|f\|^2=1\), \(F_{ab,c}=f_{abc}\), \(A=FF^T\), \(G=F^TF\), and \(S=\operatorname{Sym}_4(A)\), the full symmetrization is an orthogonal projection. Thus \(\langle A,S\rangle=h\) and \(\|S+tA\|_F^2=t^2s+(1+2t)h\).
* The derivative and Hermite product formula give \(U=3I_4(S)+12I_2(G)\), \(\mathbb EU^2=216h+288s\), and \(\kappa=1296s+1944h\).
* The sixth-cumulant regrouping in `PROOF.md` agrees with the source formula: the two additional contractions obey \(\langle FG,SF\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(A^2S)\), \(\|SF\|_F^2=\operatorname{tr}(AS^2)\), and \(\langle S,G\otimes G\rangle=(B_0+2A_0)/3\). This produces the stated coefficient \(-20\operatorname{tr}A^3\), rather than treating that term independently.
* \(\|\operatorname{Sym}_4(G\otimes G)\|_F^2=(s^2+2V_4)/3\), \(V_4\le V^{4/3}\), and \(V_4\le\sqrt{s}V\) are valid for the positive semidefinite Gram matrices. No positivity of \(S\) is used.
* The four selected pairs \((p,q)\) have \(c_p,c_q>0\) and \(\zeta\ge0\); these signs are essential when replacing traces of cubes by Frobenius norms. The assertion is made for these selected pairs, not arbitrary \(p>0,q<0\).
* Both Gram branches are valid: the first uses \(V_4\le\sqrt{s}V\), and the second projects \(S\) orthogonally to \(A\), bounds the resulting contraction by Cauchy--Schwarz, and uses \(V_4\le V^{4/3}\). The \(45ay^2\) term uses \(\|A\|_{\rm op}\le V^{1/3}\).
* Normalization yields \(\mathbb EU^2/\kappa=(1+1/d)/9\) and \(d=1+3y^2/2\). The exact coefficient conversion is \(633((1+1/d)/9-7/45)=211/(3d)-422/15\).

The positivity required when multiplying the lower bound for \(\sqrt d\) holds because the bracket has minimum \(194/\sqrt{10}-211/5>0\). The ordinary rational tangent bounds and the dedicated corner bound have the required direction. ## Connected Wick identity audit

I inspected `certify_wick_identity.py` and reran it successfully. Its enumeration covers every loopless degree-three multigraph on the labeled cumulant vertices once. The weight \((3!)^r/\prod m_{ij}!\) counts the Wick pairings of its labeled tensor slots. The connected-graph filter is the standard cumulant rule. The three expansions of an \(S\) block are precisely the three pairings in the full symmetrization of \(A\); contraction indices occur twice, without loops. Graph isomorphism merges contractions legitimately because every vertex carries the same symmetric cubic tensor. Equality of each graph-class coefficient is sufficient in every Gaussian dimension, without assuming those contractions are algebraically independent.

## Scope

At \(Q=(Z^3-3Z)/\sqrt6\), \(y=a=1\), \(\kappa=90\), \(\kappa_6=52380\), and the deficit is zero, so the claimed equality check is exact. This does not establish that the coefficient 633 is optimal. The certificate proves this coupled cumulant inequality; it does not alone prove a global cubic \(L^1\) bound, an operator sign norm, or a new Grothendieck endpoint. An infinite-dimensional third-chaos version follows from finite tensor truncation and the usual fixed-chaos moment continuity, if that extension is desired.
