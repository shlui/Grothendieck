primary `SHA256.json`, exact rational candidate, complete compact trees
on `[0,8]` and `[8,32]`, exact tail32, a checker and all 48 source corners
at `b=.7171,.713,.705`. Independent records are copied under a separate
addition manifest. The displayed boxes are independently checked regional
prices; using a gate outside a displayed box requires the actual moment
and source repricing.

The scalar replays use Arb384, exact rational physical exclusions and
independently derived exact tails. Regional prices use producer-free
directed reconstruction, exact coefficient spectral prerequisites and
separate cumulant/shape convexity. Literal reserve adapter changes and
byte-identical aliases are explicitly hash-bound. Numerical discovery
only proposes the exact candidates and is not a feasibility premise.
