## Centering, transport, and operator parameter

The fitting cumulant is \(\kappa_0=1/20\), so the local scalar comparison
must retain the frozen centering

\[
\theta=\frac1{123}.
\]

The required nuisance contribution has the plus sign

\[
+a_2[\kappa/3-\theta(\kappa+2)]
=a_2\frac{40\kappa-2}{123}.
\]

Here \(a_2<0\): the contribution is positive below \(\kappa=.05\), zero
at .05, and negative above .05. The constant nuisance has zero expectation.

The source transport
\(y(r)=y_0+p_0p_2(r-r_0)\) leaves the entire local potential unchanged.
With \(v_y=y_0/p_2-p_0r_0\), its outer source cost is exactly

\[
br^2-2(\gamma-p_0v_y)r+v_y^2.
\]

The local potential and scalar majorant do not depend on \(b\). Reducing
\(b\) from .72 weakens the even gate and both \(b\)-dependent odd gates;
the independent audit also verifies their rational margins and determinants
at both .72 and .7171. The exact price change is

\[
P_b-P_{.72}
=(b-.72)r^2+6\beta^2(1/b-1/.72)
=(b-.72)\left[r^2-\frac{6\beta^2}{.72b}\right].
\]

The coefficient \(\beta\) is the raw \(H_3\) coefficient, hence the factor
six. The two masks refer to different fixed operators \(T_b\); a global
assembly must use the same \(b\) throughout its source cover.

## Physical upper-source bound

The accepted \(\kappa\in[0,1]\) cubic L1 refinement gives
\(\mathbb E|Q|<\rho=.830644\). Its independently audited source hash
matches the ledger. For \(a=3\), the quadratic Hermite interpolant of
\(J_a(t)=\mathbb E\max(a|Z|,t)\) at 0 and a double contact
\(t=1/\rho\) is a global upper bound because \(J_a'''\le0\). Its linear
coefficient is strictly positive, as checked by directed arithmetic.
Consequently the four physical sign values and independence of \(Z,Q\)
give

\[
3u+r\le\mathbb E J_3(|Q|)
\le(1-\rho^2)3\sqrt{2/\pi}+\rho^2J_3(1/\rho)
<2.524878196278598.
\]

For \(u\ge.743\), this proves
\(r<.295878196278598<.3\). It supplies an upper source limit; it does
**not** place every physical source above a polygon's lower boundary.
