## Analytic inequality: signs and factors

Let `Q` be a normalized real third Gaussian chaos, `tau=||DQ||^2/3`, and `U=tau-1`. Gaussian integration by parts gives

\[
\mathbb E[Qv(Q)]=\mathbb E[\tau v'(Q)].
\]

Write `c=sqrt(2/pi)`. The odd solution `g` of the absolute-value Stein equation is, for `x>=0`,

\[
g(x)=-1+cR(x),\qquad
R(x)=e^{x^2/2}\int_x^\infty e^{-t^2/2}\,dt,
\quad g'(x)=-c(1-xR(x)).
\]

It satisfies `g'-xg=|x|-c`. Hence `E|Q|-c=-E[U g'(Q)]`. For an odd `C1` auxiliary function `phi` and `eta>0`, put `r=g'-phi'`. Since `E[phi'-Q phi]=-E[U phi']`, Young's inequality gives

\[
\mathbb E|Q|\le c+
\mathbb E\left[\phi'(Q)-Q\phi(Q)+\frac{r(Q)^2}{2\eta}\right]
+\frac\eta2\mathbb E U^2.
\]

Thus a whole-line majorant `q0+q2 x^2+q4 x^4+q6 x^6` for the bracket yields

\[
\mathbb E|Q|\le c+q_0+q_2+(3+\kappa)q_4
+(15+15\kappa+\kappa_6)q_6+\frac\eta2 V,
\quad V=\mathbb E U^2.
\]

Every saved row has odd weights exactly `(1,0,0)`, zero sixth offset, positive `eta`, and positive `q6`. These conditions were asserted in the independent replay. The sign of `q4` is unrestricted because the fourth moment is known exactly, rather than bounded in a direction that would require a sign assumption.

The rational auxiliaries vanish at zero; their odd extensions are `C1` and have at most polynomial growth. Their brackets are even. Checking the nonnegative half-line therefore covers the entire real line.

## Whole-half-line verifier audit

For `x>0`, Mills' estimates give `0<=1-xR(x)<=1/x^2`. The archived verifier uses those bounds only on `[64,infinity)`. In the squared residual, replacing the scalar `c(1-xR)` by the two endpoints of `[0,c_upper/x^2]` suffices because a squared affine expression is convex. The directed rational `c_upper` was independently checked to exceed `sqrt(2/pi)`.

After multiplication by a positive denominator, each endpoint gap is a rational polynomial. Translating by `64` gives nonnegative coefficients with a strictly positive constant term, proving positivity on the entire unbounded tail. The independent script rebuilt those polynomials directly from the saved rational row parameters using standard-library `Fraction` polynomial operations, without importing the archived verifier. All 16 polynomial hashes, coefficient counts, and minimum translated coefficients match the saved records.

On `[0,64]`, the archived verifier uses a cubic Taylor polynomial and a directed interval enclosure of its fourth-order remainder. Its Mills-ratio Taylor recurrence follows from `R'=xR-1`. Monotonicity of `R` gives its whole-cell interval enclosure from the two endpoints. Its recursive bisection starts from the entire compact interval, accepting a cell only when its complete Taylor lower bound is positive. There is no sampling-only acceptance step.

Every saved row was freshly passed through this verifier at 768-bit precision. The original seven rows passed with leaf counts `464,409,425,450,393,451,458`, totaling 3050; the new `k8` row passed with 432 leaves. This is a fresh replay of the archived implementation, not a second independent implementation of compact interval differentiation.
