## The six PSD gates retain the unused cubic loss

Write raw longitudinal moments `mj=E_Z[Hj(Z)k(Z,W)]`, with raw Hermites
`H0=1,H1=Z,H2=Z^2-1,H3=Z^3-3Z`. Let `rQ=P3^W m0`, `E Q^2=1`,
`t=E Q m2`, `V=p0*m0+p2*m2`. Overall oddness gives odd transverse
parity for `(m0,m2)` and even parity for `(m1,m3)`.

The exact disagreement contribution is

\[
-\|P_1^Wm_0\|^2-(\mathbb Em_1)^2+b r^2+
b\|P_2^Wm_1\|^2+\tfrac b2\|P_1^Wm_2\|^2+
\tfrac b6(\mathbb Em_3)^2.
\]

Let `E` act on `(m0,m2)` and `R` on `(m1,m3)`. The gates are

\[
E\succeq0,\quad E\succeq\mathrm{diag}(-1,b/2),\quad E\succeq pp^T;
\qquad R\succeq0,\quad R\succeq\mathrm{diag}(-1,b/6),\quad
R\succeq\mathrm{diag}(b,0).
\]

Let `Pi` be the projection onto odd transverse chaos of degree at least
three, orthogonal to `Q`. The claimed bound is exactly

\[
\mathbb E m_e^TEm_e+\mathbb E m_o^TRm_o+
(b-E_{00})r^2-2E_{02}rt-E_{22}t^2-\|\Pi V\|^2.
\]

Degree-one and degree-zero/two blocks use the displayed diagonal gates.
In degree three, `P3^W m0=rQ` has no component orthogonal to `Q`.
Subtracting the `Q`-block from the E quadratic leaves `E22` times the
squared norm of the orthogonal degree-three part of `m2`; `E22>=p2^2`
covers the same part of `||Pi V||^2`. All higher odd degrees use
`E-ppT>=0`. Remaining even degrees use `R>=0`. Thus the full-rank
replacement retains the precise physical unused loss needed for the
residual-source completion.

The residual completion therefore adds `(zeta^2/G)ppT` to E, rather than
replacing E by a rank-one matrix. Completing `-E22*t^2` yields the outer
`y^2/E22` term and the local source coefficient `q*(y-E02*r)*H2`.
`E22>=b/2>0` justifies that denominator. The source residual terms are
unchanged. The publication fragment `general_r3_extension.tex` states
and proves the complete resulting criterion.

## Physical domain, center bound, reflections, and q interpolation

The physical ellipsoid is exactly
`m0^2+m1^2+m2^2/2+m3^2/6<=1`, by longitudinal Bessel and `k^2<=1`.
The initial boxes contain it because `sqrt(2)<3/2` and `sqrt(6)<5/2`.
The restriction `m1>=0` follows by simultaneously reflecting `Z,m1,m3`;
it does not assume that two moments can be reflected independently.
Oddness of the source/local criterion reduces the scalar line to `q>=0`.
The nearest-point ellipsoid exclusion used in the producer is exact.

For any box center c and actual physical moment m, let N have blocks
`E+(zeta^2/G)ppT` and R. N is PSD. The exact center completion is

\[
J(m)\le2E_Z\max(|L|,|(b_0+Nc)^TH|)-c^TNc+g(q)^2/(4G)
+(m-c)^TN(m-c).
\]

For half-widths w, the remainder is bounded by
`sum Njj*wj^2+2abs(N02)*w0*w2+2abs(N13)*w1*w3`, exactly the producer
radius. This bounds the physical objective; it need not bound the dual
potential at every box point.

The local support function's only non-affine q dependence is cubic with
coefficient `zeta*(-1+d0/(2G))*(p0+p2*H2)`. Since
`E|H2|=4phi(1)<1`, its second derivative is bounded below by
`-12*qb*C3` on `0<=qa<=q<=qb`, where
`C3=abs(zeta*(-1+d0/(2G)))*(abs(p0)+abs(p2))`.
Combining with the independently audited rational majorant second
derivative bound gives exactly
`[(3/2)*qb*C3+max(0,-second_lower)/8]*(qb-qa)^2`.
These interpolation claims assume `0<=qa<qb`, as in the recorded covers.

## Full-E tail and transport

With `D_H=diag(1,1,2,6)`, the tail uses

\[
\mathrm{tr}(N D_H)=E_{00}+2E_{22}+R_{11}+6R_{33}
+(\zeta^2/G)(p_0^2+2p_2^2).
\]

This is a valid upper bound: pointwise
`2abs(m^T N H)-m^T N m<=H^T N H`, and hence
`Phi_E<=2E|L|+2E|b0^T H|+trace(N D_H)+g^2/(4G)`.
The existing absolute Chebyshev bounds on the Stein function and its
derivative and the bound of the clipped quadratic by its unconstrained
value give precisely the recorded degree-six tail difference polynomial.
Nonnegative coefficients after `q=64+x` establish every `q>=64`.

The independent stdlib checker `audit_general_r3_parameters_tail.py`
reads exact rational profiles without importing any producer. It passed
all six PSD gates and the q>=64 tail for both u=.70 and u=.68 profiles.
For u=.70 it also exactly matches every producer polynomial coefficient,
every shifted coefficient, and the transported r curvature.
Details/hashes are in `general_r3_parameters_tail_audit.json`.

The exact transport is `y(r)=y0+E02*(r-r0)`. Its r curvature is
`a_r=b-E00+E02^2/E22`, which is negative for both audited profiles.
Thus a vertex price requires the additional reserve
`max(0,-a_r)*(rplus-rminus)^2/4`. Dropping it is invalid.
