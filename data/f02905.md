# A continuous regional criterion for the cubic operator

This file records the analytic reductions behind the fresh profile search. The two frozen profiles cover \([.21,.30]\) and \([.30,.31]\). The first profile below concerns \(u\in[3/10,31/100]\). It is a regional bound, not an unrestricted operator norm or a new lower bound for \(G_{\mathbb R}\).

## Spectral and local comparison

For odd signs \(f,g\), put \(h=(f+g)/2\), \(k=(f-g)/2\), and rotate so \(P_1h=uZ\). Let \(W\) be the transverse Gaussian coordinates. Write \(k_j(W)=\mathbb E_Z[H_j(Z)k(Z,W)]\), \(h_j(W)=\mathbb E_Z[H_j(Z)h(Z,W)]\), where the four \(H_j\) are orthonormal Hermites. Let \(r=\|P_3^Wk_0\|_2\), and \(Q=P_3^Wk_0/r\) when \(r>0\).

Let \(M\) have even block \(E\) on coordinates \(0,2\) and odd block \(R\) on coordinates \(1,3\). The sufficient spectral gates are

\[
 E\succeq0,\quad E-\operatorname{diag}(-1,b)\succeq0,
 \quad R\succeq0,\quad R-\operatorname{diag}(-1,b)\succeq0,
 \quad R-\operatorname{diag}(b,0)\succeq0.
\]

For arbitrary real \(\ell,\beta,\delta,\nu\), define

\[
 \Gamma=\frac{(\nu-E_{02})^2}{E_{22}}-E_{00}+b-2\delta,
 \qquad s=(\delta,0,-\nu,0).
\]

Projection onto each transverse chaos, the even-block Schur complement, and the longitudinal completion \(-b(\mathbb Eh_3)^2\le2\beta\mathbb Eh_3+\beta^2/b\) give

\[
 \langle f,(P_1-bP_3)g\rangle\le
 u^2-2\ell u+\mathbb E\Phi(rQ)+\Gamma r^2,
\]

where a sufficient local potential is

\[
 \Phi(t)=\sup_{h,k}\left\{k^TMk+2t\,s^Tk
                  +2\ell h_1+2\beta h_3+\frac{\beta^2}{b}\right\}.
\]

The conditional functions satisfy \(|h|+|k|\le1\). The spectral comparison needs no additional gate involving \(\delta\).

## Directed physical-moment cover

Let \(c\) be the center of a moment box \(c\pm w\). The support inequality and exact quadratic completion imply, for every actual moment vector in that box,

\[
\begin{aligned}
&k^TMk+2t\,s^Tk+2\ell h_1+2\beta h_3+\beta^2/b\\
&\quad\le2\mathbb E_Z\max\bigl(|(Mc+ts)\cdot H|,
                         |\ell H_1+\beta H_3|\bigr)
 -c^TMc+\beta^2/b+w^T|M|w.
\end{aligned}
\]

The center expectation is the sum of two Gaussian absolute-cubic integrals. This bound has no stationary-point premise. Bessel gives \(\sum_jk_j^2\le1\), and \(|k_j|\le\mathbb E|H_j|\) gives the conservative exact coordinate bounds \(1,4/5,7/10,5/8\).

If the complete covers prove \(\Phi(t_i)\le A_i\), convexity in \(t\) proves the nodal chord bound on every interval. A tail slope at least \(2\sqrt{\delta^2+\nu^2}\) gives a valid affine extension to infinity.

The frozen profile adds \(10^{-3}I\) to the numerical \(M\), adds \(1/125\) to all nodal heights, and raises the tail slope by \(10^{-6}\). All five PSD gates and all 51 full local-node covers pass in exact/directed arithmetic. This is the completed local portion of the regional proof.

## Continuous scalar parameters

The fresh global cubic \(L_1\) theorem gives \(\rho=849/1000\). Its source bridge gives a uniform transverse amplitude \(r\le R_\rho(u)\), decreasing in \(u\). The interval \([.30,.31]\) uses \(r\le R_\rho(3/10)\). On \([.21,.30]\), the lower endpoint is in the flat branch \(u\le\sqrt{2/\pi}(1-\rho^2)\), so \(r\le R_\rho(.21)=\rho=.849\). Directed arithmetic proves

\[
 R_\rho(3/10)
 <\frac{8107245010955333}{10^{16}},
\]

so the saved final source-grid endpoint is outward.

For a fixed uncentered Stein majorant \(q_0+q_2x^2+q_4x^4+q_6x^6\), \(q_6\ge0\), the cumulant price is linear in \(\kappa\) plus a nonnegative multiple of \(\kappa^{3/2}\). For each shared shape it is convex on each saved cumulant interval. Its shape dependence is affine. Consequently two cumulant endpoints and two shared shape endpoints cover the whole rectangle, once the same whole-line majorant is certified. The saved three intervals partition \([0,90]\).

For a fixed law \(Q\), the expectation of the convex nodal target at \(r|Q|\) is convex in \(r\). On a source interval of width \(\Delta r\), adding \(\Gamma r^2\) can exceed the endpoint chord by at most \(L(\Delta r)^2/4\), provided \(\Gamma\ge-L\). The exact linear gate \(E_{00}+2\delta\le b+L\) implies this condition. The first discovery profile used \(L=4\) and six source intervals. Once the matrix is frozen, the exact Schur cost gives the sharper choice \(L=\max(-\Gamma,0)\), which is used in the final assembly. The broader second profile uses eight source intervals.

Finally \(u^2-2\ell u\) is convex, so its two amplitude endpoints cover \([3/10,31/100]\). The shift \(1/125\) in all target heights is charged once. The additional tail slope is charged by at most \(10^{-6}\rho R_\rho(3/10)\). Adding \(10^{-3}I\) lowers the even Schur source cost, which is checked exactly.
