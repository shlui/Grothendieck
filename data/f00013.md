# Rigorous maximum over κζ ≤ 36

The exact candidate generator `cubic_search/physical_shape.py` is valid for the present R3 constant prices and B0 source-plane prices, subject to the sign guards below. It is not a general rule for arbitrary functions.

Write `K₀=194/√10`, `K₁=633/9`, `z₀=2/5`. Every accepted R3 moment constant has the form

\[
F(k,z)=C+L_0k+L_1kz+B k^2
       +A(K_0+K_1z_0)k^{3/2}-A K_1k^{3/2}z
       +\tfrac94 B(kz)^{3/2},
\]

where `A=q6+residual_zeta²>0` and `B=G−d0−residual_zeta²>0`. The Stein centering/nuisance terms enter C,L₀,L₁ only. The distinction between physical shape z and the residual parameter named zeta is essential.

For fixed z, the coefficient of k^(3/2) is

\[
H(z)=A[K_0-K_1(z-z_0)]+\tfrac94Bz^{3/2}>0,
\]

because `K₀−(3/5)K₁=194/√10−211/5>0`. Therefore F is convex in k, including k=0 by continuity. For fixed k it is convex in z, since the only nonlinear z term has positive coefficient.

On the curved boundary z=36/k, k>0,

\[
F(k,36/k)=C'+L_0k+Bk^2
       +A(K_0+K_1z_0)k^{3/2}-36A K_1k^{1/2}.
\]

The second derivative is

\[
2B+\frac{3A(K_0+K_1z_0)}{4\sqrt{k}}
       +\frac{9A K_1}{k^{3/2}}\ge0.
\]

Thus the curved-boundary price is also convex. The positive q6 B0 plane price has the same expression with B=0 and A=q6≥0; its additional constant and linear terms cause no change.

Take a closed rectangle `[k₋,k₊]×[z₋,z₊]` with `k₋≥0`, `2/5≤z₋≤z₊≤1`. If `k₋z₋>36` it is physically empty. Otherwise, for each feasible k, convexity in z places the maximum at z₋ or at `min(z₊,36/k)`. On each straight boundary interval the price is convex in k; on the hyperbolic interval it is convex by the formula above. Consequently the global maximum occurs among:

- feasible rectangle corners;
- intersections `(k₋,36/k₋)` and `(k₊,36/k₊)` lying on the vertical sides, omitting division by zero;
- intersections `(36/z₋,z₋)` and `(36/z₊,z₊)` lying on the horizontal sides.

These are precisely the exact rational points returned by the helper. The proof includes every boundary transition and requires no sampled derivative check. All corner prices still require directed arithmetic, and all moment coefficients must remain bound to accepted local gates and their exact nonnegative reserve choices. The scalar source parameter κζ≤36 depends only on the physical cubic variable, so it is independent of the operator coefficient and of the local profile used to bound it.

This establishes only the κ/shape supremum of each valid moment or source price. An all-source norm certificate must still retain the full amplitude/radial partition and valid local whole-q/profile bindings.
