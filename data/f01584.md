# Reduced matrix sequences and Gaussian cubic estimates

This archive accompanies the October 1, 2026 research manuscript. The
complete independently reconstructed proof gives the unconditional
constructive lower bound `200000000/112782853 = 1.7733192119195636...`
at the single coefficient `b=.711`, with gap `.001680788081` to `1.775`.
The target `1.775` and a three-significant-digit determination remain unproved.
The older `1.773` endpoint uses an unreplayed explicit norm premise;
its missing 55-interval integration cover is not used by the new proof.

The manuscript and editable TeX are in `reduced_search/deliverables/`.
The numerical appendices follow its references. The LaTeX uses only
`amsmath`, `amssymb`, `amsthm`, and `geometry`.

## Freshly proved results

- Exact ratio-preserving weighted copies put every rational seed in a
  simultaneous symmetric, zero-diagonal, zero-sum class with entrywise
  absolute values doubly stochastic. Uniform orthogonal transformations
  do not supply that reduction.
- An exhaustive nested dyadic search gives reduced matrices with
  nondecreasing ratios converging to the full Grothendieck constant. It
  has no claimed efficient convergence rate.
- The explicit analytic Gaussian seed, followed by dyadic approximation
  and integer cloning, gives matrices whose ratios tend to `1/N(b)`.
  The radial numerator has a quantified `O(n^-2)` loss; its discretization
  contributes only `O(n^-4)`. These are unconditional numerator results.
- At `b=1/2`, the published sharp affine operator theorem of Saha et al.
  gives an exactly evaluable benchmark: `C(A_n)=11/(6*pi)` for every
  analytic seed and `lim G(H_n)=6*pi/11 = 1.713595992867...` after rational
  approximation and exact reduction. This uses a published theorem,
  independently checked here for applicability; its external numerical
  proof is not replayed by this archive.
- A formal connected-Wick identity and an exact 84-rectangle tensor
  comparison prove the coupled sixth-cumulant inequality with constant
  633 in every finite Gaussian dimension.
- Ten complete scalar Stein certificates, followed by independent
  continuous repricing, prove `E|Q| < .847` for every normalized real
  third Gaussian chaos. The original `.849` cap remains valid in all
  frozen regional certificates.
- A complete independently replayed native/R3 envelope at `b=.7171`
  proves `N(b) <= 70756627/125000000 = .566053016`. All agreement amplitudes,
  physical source amplitudes, cumulants `[0,90]` and tensor shapes are
  included. Its 39,662 middle cells are glued to complete outer branches.
  Hence the explicit reduced rational sequence has actual limiting ratio
  `1/N(b) >= 125000000/70756627 = 1.766618976905...`; the certified cap is
  not asserted to equal the actual norm.
- A strengthened complete envelope at the same coefficient proves
  `N(.7171) <= 56513883/100000000 = .56513883`, and hence the earlier
  unconditional lower bound `100000000/56513883 = 1.7694767142438257...`.
  It retains the entire original source partition, independently checks
  physical clipping and five empty cells, and includes 226 fixed contact
  improvements and nine whole-line R3 profiles.
- The preferred complete physical-polygon envelope proves
  `N(.711) <= 112782853/200000000 = .563914265`, hence the current
  unconditional lower bound `200000000/112782853 = 1.7733192119195636...`.
  The original complete upper partition has 255,360 cells and 1,400
  amplitude/cumulant groups; twenty-nine independently accepted whole-line
  R3 profiles and exact amplitude/cumulant refinements yield 272,579 priced
  cells and 8,737 exactly empty cells. The last two whole-line profiles strengthen the same final cover.
  The cumulant refinement uses a 68-point
  cumulant grid and retains all accepted parent bounds. Every changed cell,
  source polygon, native knot and concave edge maximum is replayed exactly.
  The complete lower branch at the final coefficient has cap .563307766601.
  The accepted `.713` mosaic is then transported uniformly to `.711`:
  all 272,579 cell prices, 43 profile bindings, spectral budgets and fixed
  contact square terms are independently checked. The exact final cap is
  `.563914265`; the actual sequence limit remains `1/N(.711)`.
  No historical norm premise is used. The gap to 1.775 is .001680788081.
- Seven complete continuous regional certificates at `b=.72` prove the target norm cap
  on every amplitude `0 <= u <= .682` and `.77 <= u <= sqrt(2/pi)`. Their
  local moment partitions, scalar half-lines, infinite tails and outer
  parameter prices replay without an optimizer. Earlier sharper small
  regions are retained as supplementary certificates.
- The actual cubic projection criterion has a complete, independently
  replayed physical-moment certificate for `u in [.7549,.7551]`,
  `r in [.2574,.2577]`, `kappa in [.51,.55]`, with every admissible shared
  tensor shape. Its cap `.56334 < 40/71` covers that physical rectangle;
  it does not close the entire middle amplitude range.
- Further independently accepted R3 regions cover `u in [.682,.6905]`,
  `kappa <= 1`, `r >= .2`, with cap `.563371`, plus three explicitly
  restricted full-even source bands near `u=.70`. These are parameter
  regions rather than complete amplitude intervals.
- A globally valid off-origin Hermite contact majorant retains the
  longitudinal cubic moment. Its exact derivative recurrence and rational
  coefficient roundings pass independent replay. A separate linear-head
  contact excludes physically impossible source regions. Fixed nonnegative
  multipliers can reprice accepted gates without new local integration.
- Analytic support/source comparisons retain disagreement mass,
  longitudinal cubic moments, and the actual shared variance shape.
  Both rank-one and full-rank even-block residual criteria have separate
  analytic proofs; numerical source cells require complete local and
  outer-parameter certificates in addition.
  A sharp conditional Gaussian profile yields a stronger quartic budget
  and a mixed cubic completion using one scalar source.

The stronger `40/71` all-sign norm estimate remains unresolved before
this construction can prove `1.775`. In particular, a small
fitted scalar price is not automatically an operator norm certificate.

## Witnesses, ceilings and discovery

The fresh `.72` scalar-norm **lower** witness is greater than
`.563241882518393914882`. Its reciprocal gives a construction **ceiling**
below `1.7754361511`; this is neither an achieved lower bound nor a norm
upper bound. The `.699` construction has ceiling `1.77440101857...`. A fresh exact
rational reflected-sign witness at `.7171` gives a construction ceiling
strictly below `1.775462496412`, independently replayed with a separate
Arb integration. These witnesses do not exclude a future `1.775` norm proof at `.713`
or `.7171`. A fresh reflected-sign witness at `.713` gives a construction
ceiling below `1.775407850864`. At `.705` a separate accepted witness proves
that this particular coefficient has ceiling below `1.774988761828`, so
it cannot reach the requested `1.775` within the pure cubic construction.

Files containing `discovery`, trial fits, or incomplete local trees are
research records. Their solver objectives and sampled inequalities are
not accepted by the paper as global proofs. The projection-residual
directory contains the current hardest-region work, with its own exact
local checks and explicit status records. A passed source subinterval
does not imply that the complete source or outer-parameter cover passed.

The odd-tail screen is numerical evidence about a different operator
family, `P1 - b P3 + t Podd`, where `Podd` includes every odd degree.
Its asymptotic vector numerator is `1+t`; its selected sign witnesses
give reciprocal ceilings only. They do not improve the accepted lower
endpoint.

The conditional finite-ratio calculation uses the retained denominator
`N(.699) <= 1000/1773`. For example it gives
`G(H_1024(.699)) >= 1.772998563604`. The numerator estimate is fresh;
the denominator premise is separately labeled throughout.
