# Independent audit of the frozen R3 local certificate

The local formulas, directed derivative mechanism, and interpolation budgets
pass. This is an audit of a frozen source point. It does not supply a cover of
the outer source parameters and does not prove a global operator norm bound.

The audited producer files are under
`tonight/operator/projection/certificate/`. Exact symbolic checks are recorded
in `r3_local_symbolic_audit.json`; the checker is
`audit_r3_local_symbolic.py`. It uses a separate multivariate Fraction
polynomial ring and a separate rational-polynomial representation of the
Chebyshev features. Its 28 derivative enclosure tests cover derivatives zero
through three at seven rational points, including 0, 1, and 64, for a full
16-term feature combination. They pass at 256-bit MPFR precision.

The recorded compact partition and rational tail are checked by
`replay_r3_local_cover.py`. This reconstructs the partition from its events,
checks each accepted leaf again, and regenerates the tail polynomial with
exact Fractions. It reuses the separately audited directed endpoint integral
and derivative primitives. Its result is stored in
`r3_local_partition_audit.json` when complete.

## Moment coordinates and product interpolation

The even matrix is the exact rank-one form generated by `(p0,p2)` in the
unnormalized Hermite basis `1,Z^2-1`. The reduced even dual coordinate is
`s=p0*k0+p2*k2`. Physical conditional moments satisfy

\[
 |s|\leq\sqrt{p_0^2+2p_2^2}<1,\qquad |k_1|\leq1,
 \qquad |k_3|\leq\sqrt6<5/2.
\]

Thus the initial moment box covers every physical maximizing moment. The
quadratic dual has positive diagonal coefficients
`rho=1+zeta^2/G`, `R11`, and `R33`. In each moment coordinate, its maximum
integral term is convex and the remaining quadratic has second derivative
at least minus twice the corresponding diagonal coefficient. Endpoint
interpolation therefore costs `diagonal*(width)^2/4`. Successive interpolation
gives the product-corner allowance used by the producer. Cross terms are
affine in each separate coordinate and require no additional allowance.

## Scalar q curvature

Write

\[
 \phi(q)=\frac{q}{1+q^2}\sum_j c_j
 T_j\left(\frac{q^2-1}{q^2+1}\right),\quad
 H=q^2-1,\quad \ell_0=-1-\theta H,
 \quad e=\phi'-a_0-a_2H.
\]

The clipped Fenchel price is

\[
 P(q)=\begin{cases}e^2/(2\eta),&e\geq\eta\ell_0,\\
 \ell_0 e-\eta\ell_0^2/2,&e\leq\eta\ell_0.
 \end{cases}
\]

Its value and first derivative agree at the switching set. The exact second
derivatives used in `second_lower` are

\[
 P''_{\rm in}=\frac{(e')^2+ee''}{\eta},\qquad
 P''_{\rm out}=-2\theta e-4\theta qe'+\ell_0e''
              -\eta(4\theta^2q^2-2\theta\ell_0).
\]

The scalar part after subtraction of the majorant is
`g(q)^2/(4G)-polynomial-q*phi+(1+theta*H)*phi'+P`, where
`g(q)=(c-3d0)*q+d0*q^3`. Its displayed second derivative is correct. The Jet
entries are Taylor coefficients, so multiplying entries two and three by 2
and 6 gives the actual derivatives. The convolution and inverse recurrences
are correct. Evaluating them with outward interval arithmetic on a q interval
encloses all pointwise derivatives, even when the clipped gate changes branch.

If the scalar second derivative is at least `-K`, its endpoint chord costs
`K*(width)^2/8`. The cubic source entering the maximum integral has second
derivative magnitude at most

\[
 6q C_3,\qquad
 C_3=\left|\zeta\left(-1+\frac{d_0}{2G}\right)\right|
 (|p_0|+|p_2|).
\]

Here the elementary Gaussian fact
`E|Z^2-1|=4*phi(1)<1` is used. The last inequality follows from
`pi>3` and `e>65/24`, which give `pi*e>8`. Because the maximum integral is
multiplied by two, its q semiconvexity cost is
`(3/2)*q_upper*C3*(width)^2`. Hence the complete q allowance
`[(3/2)*q_upper*C3+K/8]*(width)^2` is correct.

All maximum-integral roots serve only as proposals for exact rational
surrogate polynomials. The certificate pays the global L2 polynomial
discrepancy. Consequently no floating-point root is assumed to be exact.
The primitive Gaussian integrals and the six unordered-pair error budget have
the correct signs. Choosing a candidate polynomial separately on each
surrogate-root interval gives a valid lower integral; adding all positive
surrogate differences and the global error budget gives a valid upper one.

## Whole-q tail

For `q>=R>=1`, `|T_j|<=1` and `|T_j'|<=j^2` give

\[
 |\phi(q)|\leq A_\phi/q,\qquad
 |\phi'(q)|\leq A_\phi/q^2+4A_D/q^4.
\]

The tail uses these inequalities, the unrestricted quadratic dual's equality
with its physical-sign supremum, a positive-semidefinite trace bound for its
quadratic moment term, and the unrestricted Fenchel price `e^2/(2eta)`.
The rational difference polynomial in the producer is correct. Nonnegative
coefficients after the exact shift `q=R+t` prove it on the entire tail.

## Frozen theta and continuous kappa

For `U=||DQ||^2/3-1`, `H=Q^2-1`, and any fixed theta, let `Utheta=U-theta*H`.
Then

\[
 \operatorname{Var}(U_\theta)=V-2\theta\kappa/3+\theta^2(\kappa+2),
 \qquad \operatorname{Cov}(U_\theta,H)=\kappa/3-\theta(\kappa+2).
\]

Stein's identity yields
`E[Dtheta phi]=E[Utheta*(phi'-gamma0-gamma2*H)] + gamma2*Cov`.
Therefore the nuisance correction in the outer price has the **plus** sign.
For a frozen `theta0=kappa0/[3(kappa0+2)]` this remains exact at every nearby
kappa; reoptimizing theta is unnecessary. A negative coefficient `B*kappa^2`
requires the endpoint-chord reserve `(-B)_+*(Delta kappa)^2/4`, in addition to
any separate nonconvex pricing terms.

## Input checks and limits

For the current frozen profile, `eta>0`, `G>0`, and
`p0^2+2*p2^2<1` all hold. These should be explicit input checks in a general
region runner; the last assertion was present only in the fixed-q runner at
the start of this audit. Negative q intervals also require an absolute-q
curvature bound, whereas the current certificate is restricted to q>=0 and
uses even symmetry.

The quartic mixed-agreement extension is a separate potential. Its quartic
moment term and its changed scalar coefficient require fresh local and tail
enclosures. The audited quadratic R3 cover does not certify that extension.
