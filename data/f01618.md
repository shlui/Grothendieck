## Normalization and Stein signs

\[
\mathbb EQ^2=1,\quad \kappa=\mathbb EQ^4-3,\quad
\kappa_6=\mathbb EQ^6-15\mathbb EQ^4+30,\quad

For real third chaos the fixed-chaos integration-by-parts identity is
\(\mathbb E[Q\psi(Q)]=\mathbb E[(1+U)\psi'(Q)]\).
Let \(c=\sqrt{2/\pi}\), \(R(x)=e^{x^2/2}\int_x^\infty e^{-t^2/2}\,dt\), and
\(G(x)=1-xR(x)\) on \(x\ge0\).
The odd Stein solution for \(h(x)=|x|\) is \(f_h(x)=cR(x)-1\) on the positive half-line; it has \(f_h(0)=0\) and

\[
f_h'(x)=-cG(x),\qquad f_h'(x)-xf_h(x)=|x|-c.
\]

For an odd, continuously differentiable regression \(\phi\),

\[
\mathbb E|Q|-c
=\mathbb E[\phi'(Q)-Q\phi(Q)]
-\mathbb E[U(f_h'(Q)-\phi'(Q))].
\]

Young's inequality therefore gives

\[
\mathbb E|Q|\le c+
\mathbb E\left[\phi'(Q)-Q\phi(Q)
+\frac{(f_h'(Q)-\phi'(Q))^2}{2\eta}\right]
+\frac\eta2\mathbb EU^2.
\]

Thus the archived gap's signs and factor \(\eta/2\) are correct. If the bracket is globally bounded by
\(q_0+q_2x^2+q_4x^4+q_6x^6\), the moment price follows. Each frozen row has \(\eta>0\), \(q_6>0\), odd weights exactly \((1,0,0)\), and zero sixth offset. Its rational \(\phi\) vanishes at zero and extends oddly to a \(C^1\) function with polynomial growth, so the Stein identity applies. Its scalar gap is even, making a positive-half-line proof sufficient.
