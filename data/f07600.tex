\label{thm:global-b7171-polygon}

\[
 \N(b_*)\le B_2:=\frac{141203321}{250000000}=.564813284.

Theorem~\ref{thm:same-b7171-regions} are below $B_2$.

$z=2/5+(3/5)s$ between native and R3 coordinates.

The eleven accepted R3 profiles have $154$ cumulant-band constants.

profile selection need not be optimal.

Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:physical-source-polygon-price} to each remaining

\[
 \frac{564813282537}{10^{12}}< B_2.
\]
The frozen record is
\texttt{global\_\allowbreak middle\_\allowbreak b7171\_\allowbreak elevenbank\_\allowbreak exactpolygon\_\allowbreak envelope.json};

\texttt{polygon\_\allowbreak envelope\_\allowbreak audit.json}.

\end{proof}
